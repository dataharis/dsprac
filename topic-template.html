15-Day Teaching Methodology & Pedagogy Plan

Target: 8–9+ Marks out of 10 in DSSSB TGT Section B (Paper 2). Daily Commitment: 30–40 Minutes.

Day Module / Unit Core Topic (Concepts & Keywords) Practice Focus
Day 1 Learning Theories & Psychology Behaviorism: Thorndike's Laws, Skinner's Operant Conditioning, Pavlov. Drill & practice vs. reinforcement in lab learning.
Day 2 Learning Theories & Psychology Constructivism: Piaget's 4 Stages, Vygotsky (ZPD, Scaffolding, MKO). Collaborative project work and peer problem-solving.
Day 3 Teaching Methods & Maxims Teacher vs. Learner-Centered: Lecture, Demonstration, Heuristic, Project, Problem-Solving. Inductive vs. Deductive method & Maxims of Teaching.
Day 4 Taxonomy & Lesson Planning Bloom's Revised Taxonomy (Remember to Create), 5E Model, Micro-Teaching (36 mins). Formulating behavioral objectives & lesson steps.
Day 5 ICT & Digital Pedagogy Blended & Flipped Learning, CAI, Government Portals: SWAYAM, DIKSHA, NISHTHA. Digital tools and online repository analysis.
Day 6 TLM & Communication Edgar Dale's Cone of Experience, Classroom Communication Barriers, FIAC. Audio-visual aids and discourse analysis.
Day 7 Mid-Plan Revision & Drill Consolidate Days 1–6 Summary Charts (Theorists, Methods, Bloom's, Portals). Solve 60 Mixed Pedagogy PYQs.
Day 8 Assessment & Evaluation Formative (FOR Learning), Summative (OF Learning), AS Learning, Diagnostic & Remedial. Test validity, reliability, rubrics, portfolios.
Day 9 NEP 2020 & NCF NEP 2020 (5+3+3+4, Coding from Class 6, PARAKH), NCF 2005, RTE Act 2009. Policy norms and structural reforms.
Day 10 Inclusive Education RPwD Act 2016 (21 Disabilities), Learning Disabilities (Dyslexia, ADHD, Autism), UDL. Accommodations for diverse learners.
Day 11 Adolescent Psychology & Motivation Stanley Hall ('Storm & Stress'), Erikson (Identity vs Role Confusion), Maslow's Needs. Intrinsic vs. Extrinsic motivation and adolescent guidance.
Day 12 Curriculum & Action Research Spiral Curriculum (Bruner), Hidden vs. Overt Curriculum, Action Research Cycle. Curriculum principles and classroom research steps.
Day 13 Subject-Specific Pedagogy Pedagogy of Logical/Technical Subjects: Lab management, Debugging as diagnostic learning. Computational thinking and project evaluation.
Day 14 Full PYQ Marathon Solve 10 sets of 10 Pedagogy Questions from recent DSSSB TGT Official Papers. Positive vs. Negative tag-word elimination.
Day 15 Final Rapid Review 1-Page Cheat Sheet Review: NEP 2020 numbers, Theorist pairs, Digital Portals. 2 Timed 10-Question Mini Mocks.
