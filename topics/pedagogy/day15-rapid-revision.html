Day 15: Final Rapid Revision & Mini-Mock

Pedagogy Masterclass Finale | Cheat Sheet & 25-Question Test

Congratulations! You have reached the final day of the 15-Day Teaching Methodology & Pedagogy Masterclass. Before taking the final Mini-Mock exam, quickly review this ultimate Cheat Sheet containing the most frequently tested facts.

Ultimate DSSSB Pedagogy Cheat Sheet (अंतिम त्वरित पुनरीक्षण)

🧠 Psychologists & Theories

  • Piaget: Cognitive Constructivism (Schema, Assimilation, Accommodation). 4 Stages (Sensorimotor to Formal).
  • Vygotsky: Socio-Cultural Theory (MKO, ZPD, Scaffolding, Private Speech).
  • Skinner: Operant Conditioning (Reinforcement, Punishment).
  • Pavlov: Classical Conditioning (UCS, UCR, CS, CR).
  • Thorndike: Trial & Error (Laws of Effect, Readiness, Exercise).
  • Kohlberg: Moral Development (Pre-conventional, Conventional, Post-conventional).

📚 Acts & Policies

  • NEP 2020: 5+3+3+4 structure. 360-degree Holistic Progress Card. PARAKH assessment body. Coding from Class 6.
  • RTE 2009: Article 21A (86th Amendment). PTR: Primary (30:1), Upper Primary (35:1). Min 45 working hours/week for teachers. Sec 17 bans punishment.
  • RPwD Act 2016: 21 disabilities. 40% Benchmark. Free education up to 18 years.
  • NCF 2005: "Learning without burden" (Prof. Yash Pal). Connect knowledge to outside life.

📝 Assessment & Evaluation

  • Assessment FOR Learning: Formative (Feedback during class).
  • Assessment OF Learning: Summative (Final exams/grading).
  • Assessment AS Learning: Metacognition (Self/Peer assessment).
  • Diagnostic Test: Finds learning gaps.
  • Remedial Teaching: Fixes learning gaps.

💻 Tech & Methods

  • Edgar Dale's Cone: Base (Direct experiences - 90% retention) to Apex (Verbal symbols - 10%).
  • FIAC (Flanders): 10 Categories. Analyzes Verbal behavior only.
  • Project Method: Kilpatrick (Pragmatism). Execution is longest step.
  • Heuristic Method: Armstrong (Discovery).
  • SWAYAM: MOOC with 4 Quadrants.
  • DIKSHA: Energized Textbooks (QR codes).
Final Advice (अंतिम सलाह): When answering pedagogy questions, always choose the option that makes the student an active explorer, promotes inclusive practices, connects learning to the real world, and treats the teacher as a facilitator. Avoid options containing "only," "rote memorization," or "strict punishment."

Final Mini-Mock Exam (25 MCQs)

Test your complete pedagogical readiness. Good luck!

Score: 0 / 25

Q1. A teacher provides temporary support to a child to solve a math problem and gradually removes the support as the child becomes independent. Vygotsky termed this process:

एक शिक्षक गणित की समस्या को हल करने के लिए बच्चे को अस्थायी सहायता प्रदान करता है और धीरे-धीरे सहायता हटा लेता है। वायगोत्स्की ने इस प्रक्रिया को क्या कहा?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Scaffolding is temporary, tailored support given by an MKO within a learner's ZPD.

Q2. Under NEP 2020, the traditional 10+2 structure is replaced by:

NEP 2020 के तहत, पारंपरिक 10+2 संरचना को किसके द्वारा प्रतिस्थापित किया गया है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): 5 (Foundational) + 3 (Preparatory) + 3 (Middle) + 4 (Secondary).

Q3. "Learning without Burden" was the core theme of the report submitted by the committee headed by:

"शिक्षा बिना बोझ के" रिपोर्ट की मुख्य थीम थी। इस समिति के प्रमुख कौन थे?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The Yash Pal Committee (1993) strongly advocated reducing the school bag load, which later became the foundation of NCF 2005.

Q4. A test designed specifically to identify the exact cause of a student's repeated failure in algebra is called a:

बीजगणित में छात्र की बार-बार विफलता के सटीक कारण की पहचान करने के लिए विशेष रूप से डिज़ाइन किए गए परीक्षण को क्या कहा जाता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Diagnostic tests locate learning gaps so that remedial teaching can be prescribed.

Q5. According to Piaget, a child who can understand that $A > B$ and $B > C$ implies $A > C$ has developed the skill of:

पियाजे के अनुसार, जो बच्चा यह समझ सकता है कि A > B और B > C का अर्थ A > C है, उसने कौन-सा कौशल विकसित कर लिया है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Transitivity (developed in the Concrete Operational Stage) allows a child to logically infer the relationship between two objects based on their relationship with a third.

Q6. Which of the following defines 'Assessment AS Learning'?

निम्नलिखित में से कौन 'सीखने के रूप में आकलन' को परिभाषित करता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Assessment AS learning focuses on metacognition—students becoming aware of their own learning processes through self-assessment.

Q7. The 'Flipped Classroom' model involves:

'फ्लिप्ड क्लासरूम' मॉडल में क्या शामिल है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): It inverts traditional teaching: lectures happen at home (asynchronously), and active problem-solving happens in class (synchronously).

Q8. According to the RTE Act 2009, the mandated Pupil-Teacher Ratio (PTR) for Primary classes (1 to 5) is:

RTE अधिनियम 2009 के अनुसार, प्राथमिक कक्षाओं (1 से 5) के लिए अनिवार्य छात्र-शिक्षक अनुपात (PTR) क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Primary PTR is 30:1. Upper Primary (Classes 6-8) PTR is 35:1.

Q9. Taking away a student's recess time because they disrupted the class is an example of:

कक्षा में बाधा डालने के कारण छात्र के खेलने का समय (recess) छीन लेना किसका उदाहरण है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Negative punishment involves taking away a pleasant stimulus (recess) to decrease an unwanted behavior (disruption).

Q10. In Bloom’s Taxonomy (Revised 2001), what is the highest level of the cognitive domain?

ब्लूम वर्गीकरण (संशोधित 2001) में संज्ञानात्मक क्षेत्र का सर्वोच्च स्तर क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The 2001 revision placed 'Create' (producing new or original work) at the apex, swapping places with Evaluation.

Q11. Which method, developed by H.E. Armstrong, places the learner in the role of a discoverer?

एच.ई. आर्मस्ट्रांग द्वारा विकसित कौन-सी विधि शिक्षार्थी को खोजकर्ता की भूमिका में रखती है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): 'Heurisko' means "I find out". This method develops a scientific, discovery-oriented attitude in learners.

Q12. The RPwD Act 2016 guarantees free education for children with benchmark disabilities up to what age?

RPwD अधिनियम 2016 बेंचमार्क विकलांगता वाले बच्चों के लिए किस आयु तक निःशुल्क शिक्षा की गारंटी देता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Regular RTE covers ages 6-14, but the RPwD Act extends the right to free education up to 18 years for CwSN (Children with Special Needs).

Q13. Dyslexia is primarily associated with difficulties in:

डिस्लेक्सिया मुख्य रूप से किसमें कठिनाइयों से जुड़ा है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Dyslexia is a specific learning disability in reading. Dysgraphia is writing, and Dyscalculia is math.

Q14. In Flanders Interaction Analysis (FIAC), how many categories are specifically dedicated to recording 'Teacher Talk'?

फ्लैंडर्स अंतःक्रिया विश्लेषण (FIAC) में, 'शिक्षक वार्ता' को रिकॉर्ड करने के लिए विशेष रूप से कितनी श्रेणियां हैं?

Explanation (व्याख्या): FIAC has 10 categories: 1-7 for Teacher Talk, 8-9 for Pupil Talk, and 10 for Silence/Confusion.

Q15. According to Edgar Dale's Cone of Experience, which provides the highest learning retention?

एडगर डेल के अनुभव शंकु के अनुसार, किससे सबसे अधिक अधिगम प्रतिधारण (retention) प्राप्त होता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The base of the cone (doing the real thing) leads to up to 90% retention, whereas reading/listening (apex) leads to 10-20% retention.

Q16. What is the fundamental cycle of Action Research?

क्रियात्मक अनुसंधान (Action Research) का मूल चक्र क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The PAOR cycle is the defining structure of Action Research, aimed at solving immediate, localized classroom problems.

Q17. The 'Inductive Method' of teaching proceeds from:

शिक्षण की 'आगमन विधि' किस ओर बढ़ती है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Induction starts with multiple specific concrete examples, from which students derive a general rule or formula.

Q18. According to Maslow, which needs must be satisfied before a student can focus on self-esteem or self-actualization?

मास्लो के अनुसार, छात्र आत्म-सम्मान या आत्म-सिद्धि पर ध्यान केंद्रित कर सके, इससे पहले कौन-सी आवश्यकताएं पूरी होनी चाहिए?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Lower-level deficiency needs (food, sleep, safety) must be met before higher-level growth needs can be effectively pursued.

Q19. Under UDL (Universal Design for Learning), providing information via text, audio, and video addresses the principle of:

UDL के तहत, टेक्स्ट, ऑडियो और वीडियो के माध्यम से जानकारी प्रदान करना किस सिद्धांत को संबोधित करता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Representation refers to the 'What' of learning. Offering multiple formats ensures all learners, regardless of disability, can access the content.

Q20. In programming pedagogy, applying the 'PRIMM' framework begins with which step?

प्रोग्रामिंग शिक्षाशास्त्र में, 'PRIMM' फ्रेमवर्क लागू करना किस चरण से शुरू होता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): PRIMM stands for Predict, Run, Investigate, Modify, Make. It starts by having students read code and *predict* what it will do before running it.

Q21. A student studies hard solely to earn a new bicycle promised by his parents. This is an example of:

एक छात्र केवल माता-पिता द्वारा साइकिल का वादा किए जाने के कारण कड़ी मेहनत से पढ़ाई करता है। यह किसका उदाहरण है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): When behavior is driven by external rewards or punishments rather than internal joy or curiosity, it is extrinsic motivation.

Q22. In the context of curriculum, the unwritten rules, values, and routines taught implicitly in schools are known as:

पाठ्यचर्या के संदर्भ में, स्कूलों में अनकहे नियम, मूल्य और दिनचर्या जो निहित रूप से सिखाई जाती हैं, क्या कहलाती हैं?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The Hidden Curriculum includes the implicit social and cultural messages students absorb just by being in the school environment.

Q23. According to Kohlberg, at which stage is moral reasoning based on "Good Boy-Good Girl" social approval?

कोहलबर्ग के अनुसार, "अच्छा लड़का-अच्छी लड़की" की सामाजिक स्वीकृति पर आधारित नैतिक तर्क किस चरण में होता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Stage 3 (Conventional Level) focuses on living up to social expectations and roles to be seen as 'good' by others.

Q24. An assessment that checks whether a test measures exactly what it claims to measure evaluates the test's:

एक मूल्यांकन जो यह जांचता है कि परीक्षण ठीक वही मापता है जिसका वह दावा करता है, वह परीक्षण की किस विशेषता का मूल्यांकन करता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Validity ensures the test aligns perfectly with the intended learning objectives. (e.g., A math test shouldn't penalize poor English reading skills).

Q25. Which of the following is a key component of the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 evaluation reform?

राष्ट्रीय शिक्षा नीति (NEP) 2020 मूल्यांकन सुधार का प्रमुख घटक निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): NEP 2020 moves away from rote testing to regular, formative, competency-based assessments using a 360-degree Holistic Progress Card.
