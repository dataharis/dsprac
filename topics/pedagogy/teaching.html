⚠️ Three Distinct Meanings of "Final 10 Questions" Crucial Distinction

Never assume that the final 10 questions of every DSSSB paper automatically belong to Teaching Aptitude. Evidence-based analysis of official CBT papers reveals three distinct interpretations:

1. Final 10 Questions by Position (Q.91–100 of Section B / Discipline-5):
In standard DSSSB TGT (Natural Science, Maths, Social Science, English, Hindi) CBT papers, Section B is divided into five 20-question blocks (Discipline 1 to 5), and Questions 91 to 100 (the final 10 items of Discipline-5) consistently test Teaching Methodology / Subject Pedagogy. Similarly, in PGT (300 Qs total), the final 20 items of Section B (Q.181–200) test B.Ed. Teaching Methodology. However, in historical TGT Computer Science shifts (Aug 2021, June 2023, Feb 2024), Section B often had 95–100 pure CS technical questions, while Advt. 05/2024 (Post Code 56/24) explicitly added "including questions on teaching methodology/B.Ed." to Section B without fixing a question-number range.
2. The 10 Latest Available Questions on Teaching Concepts (By Date):
Refers to the 10 most recent verified pedagogy questions asked across DSSSB's latest 2025–2026 examination cycles (such as the November 2025 TGT shifts and February–April 2026 PRT shifts covering NEP 2020 competencies, Piagetian centration, Vygotsky's imaginative play, and RTE 2009 PTR norms).
3. Curated Practice Set of 10 High-Yield Teaching MCQs:
Refers to a rapid-revision diagnostic set of 10 high-probability questions combining verified PYQs, repeated pedagogical theories, and Computer Science classroom pedagogy to prepare you for the ~10-mark Teaching Methodology weightage in TGT CS Section B.

⏱️ 10-Minute Concept Recap (Final 10 Takeaways) Rapid Summary
  • 1. Piaget's Formal Operational Stage (12+ yrs): Abstract, hypothetical-deductive reasoning (essential for recursion, Boolean algebra & algorithm complexity).
  • 2. Vygotsky's ZPD & Scaffolding: Tasks slightly beyond independent ability solved via temporary MKO prompts (e.g., partial code skeletons).
  • 3. Assessment FOR vs. OF vs. AS Learning: For = Formative/diagnostic during instruction; Of = Summative certification; As = Student self/peer reflection.
  • 4. NEP 2020 Competency-Based Education: Shifts from rote memorization to performance tasks aligned with learning outcomes and transparent rubrics; 360° Holistic Progress Card.
  • 5. RTE Act 2009 Statutory Norms: Free & compulsory education (6–14 yrs, Art. 21A); Section 25 PTR (30:1 Primary, 35:1 Upper Primary); 45 working hrs/week.
  • 6. Maxims of Teaching: Psychological to Logical (start with student interest/readiness, then organize systematically) & Concrete to Abstract.
  • 7. Revised Bloom's Taxonomy (2001): Remember → Understand → Apply → Analyze → Evaluate → Create (designing/compiling new programs is highest).
  • 8. Inclusive & Differentiated Instruction: Equity over equality—tiered coding tasks, flexible grouping, and multi-modal visual scaffolds.
  • 9. Debugging as Constructivist Strategy: Treating syntax/logical bugs as cognitive conflict (disequilibrium) that drives schema accommodation.
  • 10. Laboratory & Rubric Assessment in CS: Evaluating practical coding via analytical rubrics (logic, edge-case testing, efficiency, readability) rather than paper recall.

1. Key Theorist vs. Theory & Core Keywords
Theorist Theory / Model Exam Keywords & CS Link
Jean Piaget Cognitive Constructivism (4 Stages) Schema, Assimilation, Accommodation, Equilibration, Centration (2–7y), Conservation (7–11y), Formal Abstract Logic (12+y).
Lev Vygotsky Socio-Cultural Theory ZPD, Scaffolding, More Knowledgeable Other (MKO), Psychological Tools (Language), Imaginative Play, Private Speech.
Lawrence Kohlberg Moral Development (3 Levels, 6 Stages) Hierarchic Integration, Pre-conventional (Punishment/Reward), Conventional (Golden Rule/Law), Post-conventional (Universal Ethics).
B.F. Skinner Operant Conditioning (Behaviourism) Reinforcement schedules, Shaping behavior, Teaching Machine, Programmed Instruction (Immediate feedback).
E.L. Thorndike Connectionism (Trial & Error) S-R bond; Primary Laws: Readiness, Exercise (Use/Disuse), Effect.
Ivan Pavlov Classical Conditioning Conditioned Stimulus (Bell) + Unconditioned Stimulus (Food) → Conditioned Response.
Albert Bandura Social Learning Theory Observational learning, Modelling (Attention → Retention → Reproduction → Motivation).
Erik Erikson Psychosocial Theory (8 Stages) Industry vs. Inferiority (6–12y School age); Identity vs. Role Confusion (12–18y Adolescence).
B.S. Bloom / Anderson Taxonomy of Educational Objectives 3 Domains: Cognitive (Head), Affective (Heart), Psychomotor (Hand). Revised 2001 peak: Creating.
Donald Schön / Paulo Freire Reflective Practice / Critical Pedagogy Schön: Reflection-in-action & on-action. Freire: Problem-posing dialogue vs. "Banking Model" of education.
2. Levels of Teaching vs. Characteristics
Level & Proponent Nature Role of Teacher & Learner CS Classroom Example
Memory Level
(Herbart)		 Thoughtless; stimulus-response drill & rote recall Teacher-dominated, authoritarian; learner is passive memorizer. Memorizing ASCII codes, shortcut keys (Ctrl+T), or reserved C keywords.
Understanding Level
(Morrison)		 Thoughtful; grasping relationships, rules & generalizations Teacher explains & demonstrates; learner comprehends & applies rules. Tracing how a `while` loop or Binary Search works on a sorted array and explaining why it is O(log n).
Reflective Level
(Hunt)		 Upper-thoughtful; open-ended problem-solving & critical inquiry Democratic; learner is active explorer; teacher is facilitator/guide. Students independently diagnosing a database bottleneck and designing a normalized schema project.
3. Formative vs. Summative vs. Diagnostic Assessment
Dimension Formative (Assessment FOR Learning) Summative (Assessment OF Learning)
Timing & Purpose During instruction; to monitor progress & adjust teaching in real time. End of unit/term; to certify mastery, assign grades & report standing.
Feedback Mechanism Descriptive elicitation (qualitative pointers for improvement). Quantitative scores, ranks, percentages, or final certification.
Verified PYQ Examples Daily homework, exit tickets, dry-run code checks, concept maps, informal quizzes. Final exams, half-yearly/quarterly exams, term board certifications.
Diagnostic vs. Remedial Diagnostic Assessment identifies specific root causes of recurring learning gaps (before or during instruction), which is immediately followed by targeted Remedial Teaching.
4. Teacher-Centred vs. Learner-Centred Methods
Feature / Method Teacher-Centred Approach Learner-Centred Approach
Core Focus Transmission of fixed content; teacher acts as sole supplier of knowledge. Active construction of meaning; teacher acts as facilitator and scaffold.
Key Methods Lecture Method, Chalk-and-Talk, Direct Scripted Instruction, Teacher Demonstration. Heuristic (Armstrong), Project (Kilpatrick), Inquiry/Discovery, Cooperative Learning, Pair Programming.
Student Agency Passive listening; uniform pace for entire class. Self-regulation, peer dialogue, differentiated pace, hands-on experimentation.
5. Assessment Terminology & Educational Frameworks
Term / Policy Exact Exam Definition & Key Facts
Validity vs. Reliability vs. Objectivity Validity: Accuracy—measures what it intends to measure (e.g., Content Validity samples syllabus skills).
Reliability: Consistency of scores across repeated testing.
Objectivity: Freedom from personal examiner bias (enhanced by rubrics/value points).
Criterion vs. Norm-Referenced Criterion-Referenced: Compares performance against fixed learning standards/rubrics (mastery).
Norm-Referenced: Ranks a student against a peer group / percentile population.
NEP 2020 (Ministry of Education) 5+3+3+4 Curricular Structure (Foundational 3–8y, Preparatory 8–11y, Middle 11–14y, Secondary 14–18y); Coding & Computational Thinking introduced from Grade 6 (Middle Stage); PARAKH national assessment centre; 360° Holistic Progress Card; NIPUN Bharat (FLN by Grade 3).
NCF-FS 2022 & NCF-SE 2023 (NCERT) NCF-FS (Oct 2022) for ages 3–8 (Panchakosha, play-based); NCF-SE 2023 (Aug 2023) for school education—emphasizes competency-based assessment, multidisciplinary learning, and computational problem-solving.
RTE Act 2009 (Enforced 1 Apr 2010) Consequence of 86th Amendment (Article 21A); Sec 21: School Management Committee (SMC - 75% parents); Sec 25: PTR (30:1 Primary, 35:1 Upper Primary); Sec 28: Bans private tuition by teachers.

🔬 Research Methodology & Honest Coverage Limitations Note Transparency Statement

1. How Questions Were Verified: We cross-checked official DSSSB recruitment notifications (Advt. 08/22 Post Code 39/22 and Advt. 05/2024 Post Code 56/24) and official CBT shift papers archived on Testbook and Adda247 spanning 2017 to April 2026. Every question labelled Verified Official PYQ carries the exact exam tier, subject, shift date, options, and official key explanation.

2. Limitations & Why CS Pedagogy Practice Questions Are Included: Because DSSSB Computer-Based Tests (CBTs) randomize item order within 20-question discipline blocks across individual candidate response sheets, public PYQ repositories record the exact exam date and shift rather than a universal serial number (1–100). Furthermore, while DSSSB's Post Code 56/24 notification explicitly mandates "MCQs on Subject Concerned including questions on teaching methodology/B.Ed." in Section B, historical TGT Computer Science shifts (2021, 2023, Feb 2024) predominantly tested technical Computer Science items with minimal standalone CS-pedagogy questions. Therefore, all 10 Computer Science classroom pedagogy questions and 5 interactive CS scenarios in this hub are transparently labelled as Newly Created CS Practice rather than disguised as historical PYQs.
