Day 10: Inclusive Education & RPwD Act

SLDs, UDL, Gifted Learners & Disabilities | Bilingual Notes

1. Inclusive Education (समावेशी शिक्षा)

Inclusive Education means all children, regardless of physical, intellectual, social, or emotional differences, learn together in the same mainstream classroom. It is based on the Salamanca Statement (1994).

  • Segregation (अलगाव): Special schools for disabled children (System excludes them).
  • Integration (एकीकरण): Disabled children in mainstream schools, but they must adapt to the existing system (System does not change).
  • Inclusion (समावेशन): The system and curriculum adapt to meet the diverse needs of EVERY child (System changes for the child).

2. The RPwD Act, 2016 (दिव्यांगजन अधिकार अधिनियम, 2016)

Replaced the PwD Act of 1995. Key highlights include:

  • Expanded List: Increased recognized disabilities from 7 to 21 conditions (including Acid Attack Victims, Parkinson's, Blood disorders like Thalassemia, and Specific Learning Disabilities).
  • Benchmark Disability: Defined as at least 40% of any specified disability.
  • Reservation: Increased from 3% to 4% in government jobs and 5% in higher education.
  • Free Education: Guaranteed for children with benchmark disabilities between 6 to 18 years.

3. Specific Learning Disabilities (SLDs) (विशिष्ट अधिगम अक्षमताएं)

Disability (विकार) Description & Symptoms (लक्षण)
Dyslexia (डिस्लेक्सिया) Reading & reading-comprehension disorder. Confusing 'b' and 'd', 'saw' and 'was'.
Dysgraphia (डिस्ग्राफिया) Writing disorder. Poor handwriting, difficulty holding a pen, spelling issues.
Dyscalculia (डिस्कैल्कुलिया) Mathematical disorder. Difficulty with numbers, calculations, and math symbols.
Dyspraxia (डिस्प्रेक्सिया) Motor-coordination disorder. Clumsiness, difficulty tying shoelaces or catching a ball.
Dysphasia / Aphasia (डिस्फेजिया) Language/Speech impairment. Difficulty expressing or comprehending spoken language.

4. Neurodevelopmental Disorders (तंत्रिका-विकासात्मक विकार)

  • ADHD (Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder): Inattention, impulsivity, and hyperactivity. (Requires short tasks, clear rules, and movement breaks).
  • Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD / स्वलीनता): Deficits in social communication and interaction. Highly repetitive behaviors, extreme reliance on routines, and hypersensitivity to sensory inputs (loud noises).

5. Gifted Learners & UDL (प्रतिभाशाली शिक्षार्थी एवं UDL)

• Joseph Renzulli’s 3-Ring Model: Giftedness is the intersection of: 1) Above-average ability, 2) Creativity (Divergent thinking), and 3) Task Commitment.
• Educational Strategy: Acceleration (skipping grades) and Enrichment (providing complex, higher-order thinking tasks). Avoid giving them "more of the same easy work."

• UDL (Universal Design for Learning): An approach to teaching that gives all students equal opportunities to learn. It provides: Multiple means of Representation (What), Action/Expression (How), and Engagement (Why).

Q1. A student frequently writes 'b' instead of 'd', and reads 'saw' as 'was'. This specific learning difficulty is formally known as:

एक छात्र अक्सर 'd' के बजाय 'b' लिखता है, और 'saw' को 'was' पढ़ता है। सीखने की इस विशिष्ट कठिनाई को क्या कहा जाता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Dyslexia is primarily a reading disorder where the brain struggles to process graphic symbols, leading to letter reversals and decoding issues.

Q2. The Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act, 2016 expanded the list of officially recognized disabilities from 7 to:

दिव्यांगजन अधिकार (RPwD) अधिनियम, 2016 ने आधिकारिक तौर पर मान्यता प्राप्त अक्षमताओं की सूची को 7 से बढ़ाकर कितना कर दिया है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The RPwD Act 2016 recognizes 21 disabilities, adding conditions like Acid Attack Victims, Parkinson's, Dwarfism, and Specific Learning Disabilities (SLDs).

Q3. A child showing signs of clumsiness, poor hand-eye coordination, and difficulty in physical tasks like tying shoelaces or catching a ball is likely suffering from:

एक बच्चा जिसमें भद्दापन, खराब हाथ-आंख समन्वय, और जूते के फीते बांधने या गेंद पकड़ने जैसे शारीरिक कार्यों में कठिनाई के लक्षण दिखाई देते हैं, वह संभवतः किससे पीड़ित है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Dyspraxia (also known as Developmental Coordination Disorder) specifically impacts fine and gross motor skills and physical coordination.

Q4. Which landmark international declaration established the framework and principles for 'Inclusive Education' globally in 1994?

1994 में किस ऐतिहासिक अंतर्राष्ट्रीय घोषणा ने विश्व स्तर पर 'समावेशी शिक्षा' के ढांचे और सिद्धांतों की स्थापना की?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The Salamanca Statement (UNESCO, 1994) is the foundational global document advocating that all children should learn together in mainstream schools regardless of differences.

Q5. According to the RPwD Act 2016, a person with a 'Benchmark Disability' is defined as someone who has at least ______ of a specified disability.

RPwD अधिनियम 2016 के अनुसार, 'बेंचमार्क विकलांगता' वाले व्यक्ति को ऐसे व्यक्ति के रूप में परिभाषित किया गया है, जिसमें किसी निर्दिष्ट विकलांगता का कम से कम ______ हो।

Explanation (व्याख्या): A benchmark disability is strictly defined as not less than 40% of a specified disability, certified by a medical authority, to avail government benefits and reservations.

Q6. Which of the following is the most defining characteristic of Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)?

स्वलीनता (Autism Spectrum Disorder) की सबसे विशिष्ट विशेषता निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सी है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): ASD is characterized by challenges in social interaction, nonverbal communication (like poor eye contact), and highly restricted, repetitive routines.

Q7. What is the fundamental difference between 'Integration' and 'Inclusion' in education?

शिक्षा में 'एकीकरण' (Integration) और 'समावेशन' (Inclusion) के बीच मूलभूत अंतर क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Inclusion is systemic change. In an inclusive classroom, the pedagogy, TLMs, and environment are modified so that every child succeeds naturally.

Q8. Dyscalculia is a specific learning disability that directly affects a student's ability to:

डिस्कैल्कुलिया एक विशिष्ट अधिगम अक्षमता है जो सीधे तौर पर छात्र की किस क्षमता को प्रभावित करती है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Dyscalculia is "math blindness." It impairs a child's ability to grasp number concepts, memorize math facts, or comprehend time and money.

Q9. A student with ADHD (Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder) is in your class. Which of the following is the BEST instructional accommodation?

आपकी कक्षा में ADHD (अवधान न्यूनता अतिसक्रियता विकार) वाला एक छात्र है। निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सी शिक्षण व्यवस्था सबसे अच्छी है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Students with ADHD struggle with sustained attention and impulsivity. Short tasks ('chunking') and controlled movement breaks drastically improve their focus.

Q10. Joseph Renzulli proposed a "Three-Ring" definition of Giftedness. Which three traits make up this model?

जोसेफ रेंज़ुली ने प्रतिभा (Giftedness) की "थ्री-रिंग" परिभाषा प्रस्तावित की। कौन-से तीन गुण इस मॉडल का निर्माण करते हैं?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Renzulli's three-ring conception asserts that true giftedness emerges only when all three traits intersect: High Ability, High Creativity, and High Task Commitment/Motivation.

Q11. Which of the following is NOT categorized as a 'Specific Learning Disability' (SLD)?

निम्नलिखित में से किसे 'विशिष्ट अधिगम अक्षमता' (SLD) के रूप में वर्गीकृत नहीं किया गया है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Cerebral Palsy is a neurological and locomotor disability affecting body movement and muscle coordination, not a cognitive/specific learning disability.

Q12. What does UDL stand for in the context of inclusive education pedagogy?

समावेशी शिक्षा शिक्षाशास्त्र के संदर्भ में UDL का पूर्ण रूप क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): UDL is a framework developed by CAST to improve teaching for ALL people based on scientific insights into how humans learn, offering multiple means of Representation, Action, and Engagement.

Q13. According to the RPwD Act 2016, what is the quantum of reservation for persons with benchmark disabilities in Government jobs?

RPwD अधिनियम 2016 के अनुसार, सरकारी नौकरियों में बेंचमार्क विकलांगता वाले व्यक्तियों के लिए आरक्षण की मात्रा क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The 2016 Act increased the quota for government jobs from 3% to 4%, and the quota for higher education admissions to 5%.

Q14. An appropriate educational intervention for a highly 'Gifted' student in a regular classroom is:

नियमित कक्षा में एक अत्यधिक 'प्रतिभाशाली' छात्र के लिए एक उपयुक्त शैक्षिक हस्तक्षेप क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Gifted students need 'Enrichment' (deeper, complex exploration of a topic) or 'Acceleration' (moving ahead). Giving them "more" busywork leads to frustration.

Q15. Dysphasia or Aphasia is a learning disability primarily associated with:

डिस्फेजिया या एफेजिया एक अधिगम अक्षमता है जो मुख्य रूप से किससे जुड़ी है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Aphasia/Dysphasia affects the brain's language centers, impairing the ability to speak fluently or understand spoken/written language.

Q16. J.P. Guilford associated 'Creative' learners primarily with which type of thinking?

जे.पी. गिलफोर्ड ने 'रचनात्मक' (Creative) शिक्षार्थियों को मुख्य रूप से किस प्रकार की सोच से जोड़ा?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Divergent thinking involves generating multiple, unique, and out-of-the-box solutions to an open-ended problem, which is the hallmark of creativity.

Q17. Under the RPwD Act 2016, free and compulsory education for a child with benchmark disability is guaranteed between the ages of:

RPwD अधिनियम 2016 के तहत, बेंचमार्क विकलांगता वाले बच्चे के लिए निःशुल्क और अनिवार्य शिक्षा किस आयु वर्ग के बीच सुनिश्चित की गई है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): While normal RTE covers 6-14 years, the RPwD Act extends the right to free education for children with disabilities up to 18 years of age.

Q18. The use of a 'Refreshable Braille Display' or 'JAWS Screen Reader' in a computer lab is an assistive technology primarily designed for students with:

कंप्यूटर लैब में 'रिफ्रेशेबल ब्रेल डिस्प्ले' या 'जाव (JAWS) स्क्रीन रीडर' का उपयोग एक सहायक तकनीक है, जो मुख्य रूप से किन छात्रों के लिए डिज़ाइन की गई है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): JAWS (Job Access With Speech) and Braille displays convert on-screen text into audio or tactile braille dots, serving visually impaired learners.

Q19. Which of the following is the most appropriate approach for evaluating a child with Dysgraphia in a final examination?

अंतिम परीक्षा में 'डिस्ग्राफिया' वाले बच्चे के मूल्यांकन के लिए निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा दृष्टिकोण सबसे उपयुक्त है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Dysgraphia physically impairs the ability to write legibly. Providing a scribe (amanuensis), extra time, or a keyboard levels the playing field without penalizing the child's actual cognitive knowledge.

Q20. When a teacher provides multiple formats (video, audio, text) of the same lesson to ensure all students can access the content, the teacher is applying which pillar of UDL?

जब एक शिक्षक यह सुनिश्चित करने के लिए कि सभी छात्र सामग्री तक पहुँच सकें, एक ही पाठ के कई प्रारूप (वीडियो, ऑडियो, टेक्स्ट) प्रदान करता है, तो शिक्षक UDL के किस स्तंभ का उपयोग कर रहा है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): "Representation" (The 'What' of learning) means presenting information in varied formats (visual, auditory, tactile) so every learner can perceive and comprehend it.
