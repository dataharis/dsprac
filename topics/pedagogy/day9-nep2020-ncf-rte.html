Day 9: NEP 2020, NCF & RTE Act 2009

5+3+3+4 Structure, PARAKH, Panchakosha & RTE Norms | Bilingual Notes

1. National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 (राष्ट्रीय शिक्षा नीति 2020)

Drafted by the committee headed by Dr. K. Kasturirangan. It aims to transform India into a vibrant knowledge society.

  • Curricular Structure: Shifts from 10+2 to the 5+3+3+4 structure.
    • Foundational (5 yrs): Ages 3-8 (Anganwadi + Classes 1 & 2). Play/activity-based.
    • Preparatory (3 yrs): Ages 8-11 (Classes 3-5). Discovery & interactive learning.
    • Middle (3 yrs): Ages 11-14 (Classes 6-8). Experiential learning in sciences, math, arts. *Coding & Vocational training starts in Class 6 (10 Bagless Days).*
    • Secondary (4 yrs): Ages 14-18 (Classes 9-12). Multidisciplinary study, critical thinking, flexibility in subject choices.
  • PARAKH: Performance Assessment, Review, and Analysis of Knowledge for Holistic Development. (A National Assessment Centre).
  • 360-Degree Holistic Progress Card (HPC): Moving away from rote-learning evaluations to a multi-dimensional card including self, peer, and teacher assessments.

2. National Curriculum Framework (NCF 2005 & 2023) (राष्ट्रीय पाठ्यचर्या रूपरेखा)

NCF 2005 (Prof. Yash Pal - "Learning Without Burden"): Based on Rabindranath Tagore's essay 'Civilization and Progress'.

  • 5 Guiding Principles (5 मार्गदर्शक सिद्धांत):
    1. Connecting knowledge to life outside the school (ज्ञान को बाहरी जीवन से जोड़ना).
    2. Ensuring learning shifts away from rote methods (रटने की प्रणाली से मुक्ति).
    3. Enriching the curriculum to go beyond textbooks (पाठ्यपुस्तकों से परे जाना).
    4. Making examinations more flexible and integrated with classroom life (परीक्षाओं को लचीला बनाना).
    5. Nurturing an overriding identity informed by democratic concerns of the country.

NCF-SE 2023 (School Education): Introduces Panchakosha Vikas (5 sheaths of human development: Physical, Vital/Pranic, Mental, Intellectual, and Spiritual) and the Panchadi pedagogy (Aditi, Bodh, Abhyas, Prayog, Prasar).

3. Right to Education (RTE) Act 2009 (शिक्षा का अधिकार अधिनियम 2009)

Emerged from the 86th Constitutional Amendment (Article 21A). Guarantees free and compulsory education for children aged 6 to 14 years (and 6 to 18 years for Children with Special Needs / CwSN).

RTE Provision (प्रावधान) Norms / Details (मानदंड / विवरण)
Pupil-Teacher Ratio (PTR) (छात्र-शिक्षक अनुपात) Primary (Class 1-5): 30:1
Upper Primary (Class 6-8): 35:1
Instructional Days & Hours (शैक्षणिक दिन व घंटे) Primary: 200 days / 800 hours
Upper Primary: 220 days / 1000 hours
Teacher Working Hours (शिक्षक कार्य घंटे) Minimum 45 hours per week (including preparation time).
Important Sections (मह महत्वपूर्ण धाराएं) • Sec 12: 25% reservation in private schools.
• Sec 17: Prohibition of physical punishment/mental harassment.
• Sec 21: Formation of School Management Committee (SMC).
• Sec 28: Teachers prohibited from private tuition.

Q1. NEP 2020 recommends shifting the traditional 10+2 structure in school education to a new pedagogical structure. What is this new structure?

NEP 2020 स्कूल शिक्षा में पारंपरिक 10+2 संरचना को किस नई शैक्षणिक संरचना में बदलने की सिफारिश करता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): NEP 2020 proposes the 5+3+3+4 design: 5 years of Foundational, 3 years of Preparatory, 3 years of Middle, and 4 years of Secondary schooling.

Q2. According to NEP 2020, vocational education and 'Coding' will be integrated into the school curriculum starting from which class?

NEP 2020 के अनुसार, व्यावसायिक शिक्षा और 'कोडिंग' को किस कक्षा से स्कूल पाठ्यक्रम में शामिल किया जाएगा?

Explanation (व्याख्या): To promote 21st-century skills, NEP 2020 mandates the introduction of vocational training, 10 bagless days, and coding starting from Class 6 (Middle Stage).

Q3. Under the RTE Act 2009, what is the mandated Pupil-Teacher Ratio (PTR) for Upper Primary classes (Classes 6-8)?

RTE अधिनियम 2009 के तहत, उच्च प्राथमिक कक्षाओं (कक्षा 6-8) के लिए अनिवार्य छात्र-शिक्षक अनुपात (PTR) क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The prescribed PTR is 30:1 for Primary (Classes 1-5) and 35:1 for Upper Primary (Classes 6-8).

Q4. What is the full form of 'PARAKH' proposed in NEP 2020 as a standard-setting body?

NEP 2020 में प्रस्तावित 'PARAKH' का पूर्ण रूप क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): PARAKH is the newly proposed National Assessment Centre tasked with setting norms, standards, and guidelines for student assessment and evaluation.

Q5. According to the RTE Act 2009, what are the minimum number of working hours per week for a teacher, including preparation time?

RTE अधिनियम 2009 के अनुसार, एक शिक्षक के लिए तैयारी के समय सहित प्रति सप्ताह काम के न्यूनतम घंटे कितने हैं?

Explanation (व्याख्या): RTE norms mandate a minimum of 45 working hours per week for teachers, which includes time spent teaching in class and preparing lessons.

Q6. Which Section of the RTE Act 2009 strictly prohibits physical punishment and mental harassment of a child?

RTE अधिनियम 2009 की कौन-सी धारा किसी बच्चे के शारीरिक दंड और मानसिक उत्पीड़न पर पूरी तरह रोक लगाती है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Section 17 explicitly bans corporal punishment and mental harassment, making it a punishable offense.

Q7. The National Curriculum Framework (NCF) 2005 derives its educational philosophy largely from an essay titled "Civilization and Progress" written by:

राष्ट्रीय पाठ्यचर्या रूपरेखा (NCF) 2005 ने अपना शैक्षिक दर्शन मुख्य रूप से "सभ्यता और प्रगति" नामक निबंध से प्राप्त किया है। इसके लेखक कौन हैं?

Explanation (व्याख्या): NCF 2005 begins with a quote from Tagore's essay 'Civilization and Progress', emphasizing that creative spirit and generous joy are key in childhood.

Q8. According to NCF 2005, which of the following is ONE of the five guiding principles for curriculum development?

NCF 2005 के अनुसार, पाठ्यक्रम विकास के लिए पांच मार्गदर्शक सिद्धांतों में से एक कौन-सा है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The 5 principles aim to make learning relevant, shift away from rote methods, enrich the curriculum beyond textbooks, make exams flexible, and nurture a democratic identity.

Q9. Under the RTE Act, the free and compulsory education is guaranteed for normal children in the age group of 6-14 years. However, for Children with Special Needs (CwSN), the age group is expanded to:

RTE अधिनियम के तहत, सामान्य बच्चों के लिए आयु 6-14 वर्ष है। विशेष आवश्यकता वाले बच्चों (CwSN) के लिए यह आयु वर्ग कितना है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): As per the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act integration with RTE, children with benchmark disabilities have the right to free education up to 18 years of age.

Q10. Which constitutional amendment introduced Article 21A, making Right to Education a fundamental right in India?

किस संवैधानिक संशोधन ने अनुच्छेद 21A पेश किया, जिससे भारत में शिक्षा का अधिकार एक मौलिक अधिकार बन गया?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The 86th Constitutional Amendment Act of 2002 inserted Article 21A into the Constitution, guaranteeing free and compulsory education as a fundamental right.

Q11. The NEP 2020 proposes a "360-degree multidimensional report card". What does this imply?

NEP 2020 "360-डिग्री बहुआयामी रिपोर्ट कार्ड" का प्रस्ताव करता है। इसका क्या अर्थ है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The 360-degree Holistic Progress Card (HPC) evaluates cognitive, affective, and psychomotor domains using feedback from the student, their classmates, and teachers.

Q12. What are the minimum instructional days mandated by the RTE Act 2009 for Upper Primary (Classes 6-8) per academic year?

RTE अधिनियम 2009 द्वारा उच्च प्राथमिक (कक्षा 6-8) के लिए प्रति शैक्षणिक वर्ष कितने न्यूनतम शैक्षणिक दिन अनिवार्य हैं?

Explanation (व्याख्या): For Primary (1-5), it is 200 days (800 hours). For Upper Primary (6-8), it is 220 days (1000 hours).

Q13. Section 21 of the RTE Act mandates the formation of which body to monitor the working of the school and prepare the school development plan?

RTE अधिनियम की धारा 21 स्कूल के कामकाज की निगरानी और स्कूल विकास योजना तैयार करने के लिए किस निकाय के गठन को अनिवार्य करती है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The SMC (School Management Committee) must be formed in every government/aided school, comprising 75% parents/guardians, to ensure local community involvement.

Q14. In the recently released NCF-SE 2023, the holistic development of a child is mapped across five domains/sheaths known as:

हाल ही में जारी NCF-SE 2023 में, बच्चे के समग्र विकास को पांच क्षेत्रों/कोशों में मैप किया गया है, जिसे क्या कहा जाता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): NCF 2023 integrates ancient Indian philosophy, focusing on Panchakosha (Annamaya, Pranamaya, Manomaya, Vijnanamaya, and Anandamaya Kosha) for holistic growth.

Q15. According to NEP 2020, what is the age group covered under the 'Foundational Stage' (the '5' in 5+3+3+4)?

NEP 2020 के अनुसार, 'बुनियादी चरण' (Foundational Stage - 5+3+3+4 में '5') के अंतर्गत कौन-सा आयु वर्ग आता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The 5-year Foundational stage covers ages 3 to 8, which includes 3 years of Anganwadi/Preschool and 2 years of Primary school (Classes 1 & 2).

Q16. Section 28 of the RTE Act 2009 explicitly prohibits teachers from:

RTE अधिनियम 2009 की धारा 28 शिक्षकों को स्पष्ट रूप से किस बात से रोकती है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Section 28 ensures that government teachers do not engage in private tuitions to maintain focus and ethics in their school teaching duties.

Q17. Under the NCF-SE 2023, 'Panchadi' refers to a five-step process for:

NCF-SE 2023 के तहत, 'पंचादी' (Panchadi) किस चीज़ की पांच-चरणीय प्रक्रिया को संदर्भित करता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Panchadi is a traditional 5-step pedagogy approach: Aditi (Introduction), Bodh (Understanding), Abhyas (Practice), Prayog (Application), and Prasar (Expansion/Sharing).

Q18. The NCF 2005 was chaired by which prominent academician?

NCF 2005 की अध्यक्षता किस प्रमुख शिक्षाविद ने की थी?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Prof. Yash Pal headed the National Steering Committee for NCF 2005, drawing heavily from his 1993 report "Learning without Burden" (शिक्षा बिना बोझ के).

Q19. Section 12 of the RTE Act 2009 mandates what percentage of reservation in private unaided schools for children belonging to weaker sections and disadvantaged groups?

RTE अधिनियम 2009 की धारा 12 के तहत, निजी गैर-सहायता प्राप्त स्कूलों में कमजोर वर्गों के बच्चों के लिए कितने प्रतिशत आरक्षण अनिवार्य है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Section 12(1)(c) mandates private unaided schools to reserve at least 25% of their entry-level seats for EWS (Economically Weaker Section) and disadvantaged groups.

Q20. When was the Right to Education (RTE) Act 2009 officially implemented in India?

भारत में शिक्षा का अधिकार (RTE) अधिनियम 2009 आधिकारिक तौर पर कब लागू किया गया था?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Although passed by Parliament in 2009, the RTE Act came into force across India (except J&K at the time) on April 1, 2010.
