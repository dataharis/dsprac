Day 8: Assessment & Evaluation

Formative vs. Summative, Diagnostic Tests, CCE & Rubrics | Bilingual Notes

1. Assessment FOR, OF, and AS Learning (सीखने के लिए, का, और के रूप में आकलन)

Type of Assessment (प्रकार) Purpose & Timing (उद्देश्य और समय) Example (उदाहरण)
Assessment FOR Learning
(सीखने के लिए आकलन)		 Formative (रचनात्मक): Takes place during the learning process. Used to diagnose gaps and improve teaching. (Feedback-driven). Quizzes, class discussions, teacher observing group work. (Chef tasting the soup while cooking).
Assessment OF Learning
(सीखने का आकलन)		 Summative (योगात्मक): Takes place at the end of the term/unit. Used to grade, certify, or promote students. Annual exams, Board exams, Final projects. (Guest tasting the soup when served).
Assessment AS Learning
(सीखने के रूप में आकलन)		 Metacognitive (मेटाकॉग्निटिव): Students assess their own learning. Fosters self-regulation and peer feedback. Self-assessment, peer-assessment, reflective journals.

2. Diagnostic Assessment & Remedial Teaching (निदानात्मक आकलन एवं उपचारात्मक शिक्षण)

Diagnostic Test (निदानात्मक परीक्षण): Conducted to find out the specific learning difficulties, weaknesses, or root causes of a student's errors. (Like a doctor diagnosing a disease via a blood test).

Remedial Teaching (उपचारात्मक शिक्षण): The instructional strategy applied after the diagnostic test to fix the identified learning gaps. (Like the medicine given by the doctor).

Correct Sequence (सही क्रम): Formative Assessment → Diagnostic Test (finds the gap) → Remedial Teaching (fixes the gap) → Re-evaluation.

3. Qualities of a Good Test (एक अच्छे परीक्षण की विशेषताएं)

  • Validity (वैधता): The test measures exactly what it claims to measure. (A math test should not test reading comprehension).
  • Reliability (विश्वसनीयता): Consistency of scores. If a student takes the test multiple times under similar conditions, the score remains the same.
  • Objectivity (वस्तुनिष्ठता): The score is not affected by the personal bias or mood of the examiner. (e.g., MCQs are highly objective).
  • Usability/Practicability (व्यावहारिकता): Easy to administer, cost-effective, and time-efficient.

4. Assessment Tools & Frameworks (आकलन उपकरण)

  • Rubrics (रूब्रिक्स): A scoring guide used to evaluate performance, a product, or a project based on specific criteria.
  • Portfolio (पोर्टफोलियो): A purposeful collection of a student's best works, art, and assignments over a period of time, showing growth.
  • Anecdotal Record (उपाख्यानात्मक रिकॉर्ड): A teacher's brief written notes detailing specific, significant behavioral incidents of a student.
  • CCE (Continuous & Comprehensive Evaluation / सतत एवं व्यापक मूल्यांकन): Introduced by CBSE/RTE to evaluate both Scholastic (academic) and Co-scholastic (sports, arts, values) domains continuously.

Q1. A teacher tastes the food while cooking to check the seasoning. In the context of educational evaluation, this is analogous to:

एक रसोइया खाना पकाते समय चखता है। शैक्षिक मूल्यांकन के संदर्भ में, यह किसके समान है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Tasting soup while cooking allows the chef to make improvements (add salt). This is exactly what Formative Assessment (Assessment FOR learning) does—it provides feedback to improve teaching/learning while it is still happening.

Q2. Which type of assessment is primarily used for grading, certifying, and promoting students at the end of an instructional unit?

किस प्रकार के आकलन का उपयोग मुख्य रूप से एक शिक्षण इकाई के अंत में छात्रों को ग्रेड देने और कक्षोन्नति (promotions) के लिए किया जाता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Summative Assessment (Assessment OF Learning) occurs at the end of the term (like board exams) to evaluate overall student achievement and assign grades.

Q3. If a test consistently yields the same score when administered to the same student under identical conditions, the test is said to be:

यदि कोई परीक्षण समान परिस्थितियों में एक ही छात्र पर बार-बार प्रशासित किए जाने पर समान अंक देता है, तो उस परीक्षण को क्या कहा जाता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Reliability refers to the consistency and stability of test scores. Validity refers to whether the test measures what it claims to measure.

Q4. What is the primary purpose of a 'Diagnostic Test' in a classroom?

कक्षा में 'निदानात्मक परीक्षण' का प्राथमिक उद्देश्य क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Just like a doctor diagnoses a disease, a teacher uses a diagnostic test to find the exact weakness or misconception a student is facing so that remedial teaching can be planned.

Q5. After conducting a diagnostic test and identifying that a student struggles with fractions, the teacher provides specific, customized instruction to help the student overcome this difficulty. This is called:

निदानात्मक परीक्षण करने के बाद, शिक्षक छात्र को कठिनाई दूर करने के लिए विशिष्ट निर्देश प्रदान करता है। इसे क्या कहा जाता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Remedial teaching strictly follows diagnostic testing. It is the 'cure' or 'medicine' applied to fix the specific learning gap found during diagnosis.

Q6. When students are given opportunities to evaluate their own work and reflect on their learning process, it is known as:

जब छात्रों को अपने स्वयं के काम का मूल्यांकन करने और अपनी सीखने की प्रक्रिया पर विचार करने का अवसर दिया जाता है, तो इसे किस रूप में जाना जाता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Assessment AS learning focuses on the student's metacognition. It involves self-assessment and peer-assessment where the student actively monitors their own progress.

Q7. A teacher keeps a notebook where she writes down brief, factual descriptions of significant behavioral incidents regarding specific students (e.g., "Rahul shared his lunch today"). This tool is a/an:

एक शिक्षक नोटबुक में विशिष्ट छात्रों की महत्वपूर्ण व्यवहारिक घटनाओं का संक्षिप्त, तथ्यात्मक विवरण लिखती है। यह उपकरण क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Anecdotal records are short, objective, narrative summaries of specific events or behaviors observed by the teacher in the natural classroom setting.

Q8. Which of the following best defines a 'Portfolio' in educational assessment?

शैक्षिक आकलन में 'पोर्टफोलियो' को कौन सर्वोत्तम रूप से परिभाषित करता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): A portfolio is a curated folder of a student's best artifacts (drawings, essays, projects) collected over a semester or year to showcase their longitudinal growth.

Q9. The concept of CCE (Continuous and Comprehensive Evaluation) primarily stresses upon:

CCE (सतत एवं व्यापक मूल्यांकन) की अवधारणा मुख्य रूप से किस पर जोर देती है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): CCE aims for the all-round development of the child. 'Comprehensive' means evaluating both Scholastic (Math, Science) and Co-Scholastic (Sports, Art, Life Skills) domains.

Q10. A scoring guide that explicitly details the criteria for grading a project or assignment across different levels of quality is called a:

एक स्कोरिंग गाइड जो किसी प्रोजेक्ट को अलग-अलग गुणवत्ता स्तरों पर ग्रेड करने के मानदंडों का स्पष्ट रूप से विवरण देती है, क्या कहलाती है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): A rubric is a matrix or grid used by teachers (and students) that defines what is expected in an assignment and how it will be graded across various performance levels (e.g., Poor, Good, Excellent).

Q11. A mathematics test designed to measure algebra skills unintentionally contains highly complex English vocabulary, causing ESL (English Second Language) students to fail. This test lacks:

बीजगणित कौशल को मापने के लिए डिज़ाइन किए गए परीक्षण में गलती से अत्यधिक जटिल अंग्रेजी शब्दावली शामिल हो गई। इस परीक्षण में किस चीज़ की कमी है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The test is supposed to measure Math, but it is actually measuring English reading skills. Because it does not measure what it claims to measure, it lacks Validity (वैधता).

Q12. The NEP 2020 proposes a shift in the assessment system from summative and rote-memorization based testing to:

NEP 2020 मूल्यांकन प्रणाली में योगात्मक और रटने-आधारित परीक्षण से किस ओर बदलाव का प्रस्ताव करता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): NEP 2020 shifts the focus to regular, formative, and competency-based assessment to promote learning and development, testing analysis, critical thinking, and conceptual clarity.

Q13. In Norm-Referenced Testing (NRT), a student's performance is compared to:

मानक-संदर्भित परीक्षण (NRT) में, किसी छात्र के प्रदर्शन की तुलना किससे की जाती है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): NRT (like Percentiles in competitive exams like JEE/CAT) compares a test-taker to their peers (the 'norm' group). CRT (Criterion-Referenced) compares performance to a fixed standard (like getting 33% to pass).

Q14. The 360-degree Holistic Progress Card proposed by NEP 2020 will include evaluation by:

NEP 2020 द्वारा प्रस्तावित 360-डिग्री समग्र प्रगति कार्ड (HPC) में किसके द्वारा मूल्यांकन शामिल होगा?

Explanation (व्याख्या): A 360-degree evaluation means a holistic, multi-dimensional report card reflecting the student's progress evaluated by the student themselves, their peers, and their teachers.

Q15. An objective type test (like MCQs) is superior to an essay type test primarily because it possesses high:

एक वस्तुनिष्ठ प्रकार का परीक्षण (जैसे MCQ) निबंध प्रकार के परीक्षण से मुख्य रूप से बेहतर है क्योंकि इसमें उच्च ______ होता है:

Explanation (व्याख्या): MCQs remove the examiner's personal bias. Whether Examiner A checks it or Examiner B checks it, the score remains identical, ensuring high objectivity and scoring reliability.

Q16. Which type of test is most appropriate if a teacher wants to identify why a student is constantly making errors in carrying over numbers during addition?

यदि शिक्षक यह पहचानना चाहता है कि छात्र जोड़ (addition) में हासिल (carry-over) लेने में बार-बार गलती क्यों कर रहा है, तो कौन-सा परीक्षण सबसे उपयुक्त है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Diagnostic tests are designed specifically to pinpoint the root cause of a student's learning difficulty so that it can be remedied.

Q17. In the context of assessment, what does 'Ipsative Assessment' refer to?

आकलन के संदर्भ में, 'इप्सेटिव असेसमेंट' (Ipsative Assessment) का क्या अर्थ है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Ipsative assessment measures personal progress. The student is competing against themselves, not against their peers (Norm) or a fixed grade (Criterion).

Q18. Formative assessment is characterized by:

रचनात्मक आकलन (Formative Assessment) की विशेषता क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The primary goal of formative assessment is not to judge, but to form and shape the learning process through continuous, low-stakes feedback.

Q19. Which of the following is NOT a tool used for Formative Assessment?

निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा रचनात्मक आकलन के लिए प्रयुक्त उपकरण नहीं है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Annual Board Examinations are the classic example of Summative Assessment (Assessment OF Learning), occurring at the end of the year for certification.

Q20. When a student uses a rubric to grade their own essay before submitting it to the teacher, they are engaging in:

जब कोई छात्र अपना निबंध शिक्षक को सौंपने से पहले उसे ग्रेड देने के लिए रूब्रिक का उपयोग करता है, तो वह किसमें संलग्न है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Self-assessment, reflecting on one's own work, and monitoring one's own progress is defined as Assessment AS Learning (metacognition).
