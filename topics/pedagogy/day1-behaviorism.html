Day 1: Behaviorism & Learning Theories

DSSSB TGT Section B | Teaching Methodology Masterclass

Behaviorism (व्यवहारवाद) is a foundational learning theory in educational psychology that views learning as a change in observable behavior caused by environmental stimuli and reinforcement consequences, treating internal cognitive processes as a secondary "black box."

1. Ivan Pavlov: Classical Conditioning (शास्त्रीय अनुबंधन सिद्धांत)

  • Core Concept: Associative learning involving involuntary reflex responses paired with neutral stimuli.
  • Key Components: Unconditioned Stimulus (UCS - भोजन), Unconditioned Response (UCR - लार आना), Conditioned Stimulus (CS - घंटी), and Conditioned Response (CR).
  • Classroom Implication: Explains emotional conditioning, test anxiety (pairing exams with negative feedback), and environmental triggers.

2. E.L. Thorndike: Connectionism & Trial-and-Error (संबंधवाद एवं प्रयास-त्रुटि सिद्धांत)

  • Law of Effect (प्रभाव का नियम): Actions followed by satisfaction or reward are strengthened; actions followed by discomfort are weakened. (Foundation of reinforcement).
  • Law of Exercise (अभ्यास का नियम): Divided into Law of Use (practice strengthens bonds) and Law of Disuse (neglect weakens them).
  • Law of Readiness (तत्परता का नियम): A learner must be physiologically and mentally prepared to learn for satisfaction to occur.

3. B.F. Skinner: Operant Conditioning (क्रियाप्रसूत अनुबंधन सिद्धांत)

  • Core Concept: Voluntary behavior is shaped, maintained, or eliminated by its consequences (reinforcement vs. punishment).
  • Reinforcement (पुनर्बलन): Always increases the probability of a behavior recurring. Positive = adding a pleasant stimulus (praise); Negative = removing an aversive stimulus.
  • Punishment (दंड): Always decreases behavior. Positive = adding an aversive stimulus; Negative = withdrawing a privilege.
  • Classroom Application: Programmed instruction, token economies, immediate feedback loops, and sequential behavioral shaping.
High-Yield Exam Trap (परीक्षा की दृष्टि से महत्वपूर्ण): Negative reinforcement is not punishment. Negative reinforcement increases target behaviors by taking away an annoying condition (e.g., stopping a loud buzzer when a task is completed correctly).
नकारात्मक पुनर्बलन (Negative Reinforcement) कोई दंड नहीं है; यह किसी कष्टदायक स्थिति को हटाकर वांछित व्यवहार को बढ़ाता है।
