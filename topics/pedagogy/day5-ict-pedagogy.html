Day 5: ICT & Digital Pedagogy

Blended Learning, CAI, DIKSHA & SWAYAM | Bilingual Notes

1. Modern Learning Environments (आधुनिक अधिगम परिवेश)

  • Blended Learning (मिश्रित अधिगम): Combines traditional face-to-face classroom instruction with online digital learning components. Students have some control over time, place, path, or pace.
  • Flipped Classroom (फ्लिप्ड क्लासरूम): Inverts traditional teaching methods.
    At Home: Students watch video lectures (Instruction/LOTS).
    In Class: Students engage in problem-solving, projects, and discussions with the teacher's active scaffolding (HOTS).

2. Synchronous vs. Asynchronous Learning (तुल्यकालिक बनाम अतुल्यकालिक अधिगम)

Feature (विशेषता) Synchronous Learning (तुल्यकालिक) Asynchronous Learning (अतुल्यकालिक)
Timing (समय) Real-time, live interaction (वास्तविक समय में). Self-paced, not in real-time (स्व-गति, कभी भी).
Tools/Examples (उदाहरण) Zoom, Google Meet, Live Webinars, Live Chat. Pre-recorded video lectures, Emails, Discussion Forums, Blogs.
Advantage (लाभ) Immediate feedback and direct engagement. Flexibility, highly convenient for remote learners.

3. Computer-Assisted Instruction (CAI / कंप्यूटर-सहायक अनुदेशन)

CAI uses computers to provide interactive instruction directly to students. Types of CAI modes include:

  • Drill & Practice (अभ्यास): Helps learners master previously taught concepts through repetitive exercises (e.g., math flashcards).
  • Tutorials (ट्यूटोरियल): Teaches new concepts directly through text, audio, video, and quizzes. Acts like a virtual teacher.
  • Simulation (सिमुलेशन / अनुरूपण): Replicates real-life situations virtually (e.g., flight simulators, virtual science labs). Excellent for dangerous or expensive experiments.
  • Instructional Games (शैक्षिक खेल): Adds competition and rules to make learning fun and motivating.

4. Key Government Digital Initiatives (भारत सरकार की प्रमुख डिजिटल पहलें)

• SWAYAM: Study Webs of Active-Learning for Young Aspiring Minds. India's national MOOC (Massive Open Online Courses) platform providing free education from Class 9 to Post-Graduation.
4 Quadrants of SWAYAM: 1. Video lectures 2. Specially prepared reading material 3. Self-assessment tests/quizzes 4. Online discussion forum.

• DIKSHA: Digital Infrastructure for Knowledge Sharing. Offers 'Energized Textbooks' via QR codes.

• NISHTHA: National Initiative for School Heads' and Teachers' Holistic Advancement. A capacity-building program for improving quality of school education through Integrated Teacher Training.

• SWAYAM PRABHA: A group of DTH channels devoted to telecasting high-quality educational programs 24x7 using the GSAT-15 satellite.

Q1. A pedagogical model in which typical lecture and homework elements of a course are reversed—where students watch video lectures at home and solve problems in class—is called:

वह शैक्षणिक मॉडल जिसमें छात्र घर पर वीडियो व्याख्यान देखते हैं और कक्षा में समस्याओं का समाधान करते हैं, क्या कहलाता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The Flipped Classroom inverts traditional teaching. Lower-order thinking (listening to lectures) is done at home, while higher-order thinking (application, analysis) is done in class with teacher support.

Q2. Which of the following is the BEST example of 'Synchronous' online learning?

निम्नलिखित में से 'तुल्यकालिक' (Synchronous) ऑनलाइन अधिगम का सबसे अच्छा उदाहरण कौन-सा है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Synchronous learning happens in real-time. Live classes, Zoom meetings, and live chats are synchronous because participants are engaged simultaneously.

Q3. MOOC stands for:

MOOC का पूर्ण रूप क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): MOOC stands for Massive Open Online Course, which are free online courses available for anyone to enroll in, providing an affordable and flexible way to learn new skills.

Q4. In the context of CAI (Computer Assisted Instruction), which mode is most suitable for providing a safe environment to practice dangerous science experiments (like radioactive material handling)?

CAI के संदर्भ में, खतरनाक विज्ञान प्रयोगों का अभ्यास करने के लिए सबसे उपयुक्त मोड कौन-सा है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Simulation models real-life or hazardous situations virtually, allowing students to learn through safe experimentation without real-world risks.

Q5. What is the primary purpose of the 'DIKSHA' portal launched by the Ministry of Education?

शिक्षा मंत्रालय द्वारा शुरू किए गए 'DIKSHA' पोर्टल का मुख्य उद्देश्य क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): DIKSHA (Digital Infrastructure for Knowledge Sharing) offers highly interactive digital content linked to physical textbooks via QR codes for teachers and students across India.

Q6. Which of the following is NOT one of the 4 Quadrants of the SWAYAM MOOC platform?

निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा SWAYAM MOOC प्लेटफॉर्म के 4 चतुर्थांशों (Quadrants) में से एक नहीं है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The 4 Quadrants of SWAYAM are: 1) Video lecture, 2) Specially prepared reading material, 3) Self-assessment tests through quizzes, and 4) Online discussion forums. It does not track physical attendance.

Q7. An educational setup that integrates face-to-face classroom teaching with digital online activities is known as:

वह शैक्षिक व्यवस्था जो पारंपरिक कक्षा शिक्षण को डिजिटल ऑनलाइन गतिविधियों के साथ एकीकृत करती है, क्या कहलाती है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Blended learning (Hybrid learning) combines the best of both worlds: face-to-face teacher interaction and online self-paced digital tasks.

Q8. What does 'NISHTHA' stand for in the context of Indian educational initiatives?

भारतीय शैक्षिक पहलों के संदर्भ में 'NISHTHA' का पूर्ण रूप क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): NISHTHA is the world's largest teacher training program designed to build the capacities of school heads and elementary teachers across India.

Q9. Which mode of CAI is designed to help students master basic facts (like multiplication tables) through repetitive question-and-answer cycles?

CAI का कौन-सा मोड बार-बार प्रश्न-उत्तर चक्रों के माध्यम से बुनियादी तथ्यों में महारत हासिल करने के लिए बनाया गया है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Drill & Practice software assumes the concept is already taught, and provides repetitive practice to increase speed, accuracy, and fluency.

Q10. Under the TPACK framework for effective technology integration in classrooms, what does 'PK' stand for?

कक्षाओं में प्रभावी तकनीकी एकीकरण के लिए TPACK ढांचे में, 'PK' का क्या अर्थ है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): TPACK stands for Technological Pedagogical Content Knowledge. PK (Pedagogical Knowledge) refers to the teacher's deep knowledge about the processes and practices of teaching and learning.

Q11. Which government initiative utilizes GSAT-15 satellite to telecast high-quality educational programs 24x7 through DTH channels?

कौन-सी सरकारी पहल GSAT-15 उपग्रह का उपयोग करके 24x7 DTH चैनलों के माध्यम से शैक्षिक कार्यक्रमों का प्रसारण करती है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): SWAYAM PRABHA is a group of DTH channels broadcasting educational content directly to homes, ensuring students without high-speed internet can still learn.

Q12. What is the fundamental difference between CAI (Computer-Assisted Instruction) and CMI (Computer-Managed Instruction)?

CAI और CMI के बीच मूलभूत अंतर क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): CAI interacts with the student to teach concepts (like a tutorial). CMI is an administrative tool used by teachers to manage tests, track progress, and organize the curriculum.

Q13. E-Pathshala is an initiative developed by which organization to provide educational e-resources?

ई-पाठशाला शैक्षिक ई-संसाधन प्रदान करने के लिए किस संगठन द्वारा विकसित एक पहल है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): E-Pathshala is a joint initiative of the Ministry of Education (MoE) and NCERT to showcase and disseminate all educational e-resources including textbooks, audio, video, and periodicals.

Q14. In a Flipped Classroom environment, how does Bloom’s Taxonomy map to the learning phases?

फ्लिप्ड क्लासरूम परिवेश में, ब्लूम की टैक्सोनॉमी को कैसे लागू किया जाता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The major advantage of the flipped classroom is that the teacher is physically present to scaffold complex, high-level problem solving (Apply, Analyze) during class time, leaving the easy passive listening for home.

Q15. The 'Station Rotation Model' is a highly popular implementation of which type of learning?

'स्टेशन रोटेशन मॉडल' किस प्रकार के अधिगम का एक लोकप्रिय तरीका है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): In the Station Rotation model of Blended Learning, students rotate on a fixed schedule between different learning stations, at least one of which is an online learning station.

Q16. Which ICT tool is most appropriate for 'Asynchronous' discussion among learners?

शिक्षार्थियों के बीच 'अतुल्यकालिक' (Asynchronous) चर्चा के लिए कौन-सा ICT उपकरण सबसे उपयुक्त है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Discussion forums allow students to read and reply to messages at their own convenience (not in real-time), making them asynchronous.

Q17. In the TPACK model introduced by Mishra and Koehler (2006), the intersection of all three knowledge bases (TCK, TPK, PCK) results in:

TPACK मॉडल में, सभी तीनों ज्ञान आधारों (Technological, Pedagogical, Content) के प्रतिच्छेदन (intersection) से क्या प्राप्त होता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): TPACK represents the 'sweet spot' where a teacher knows their subject matter (Content), knows how to teach it (Pedagogy), and knows the best digital tools to enhance that specific lesson (Technology).

Q18. What is the main characteristic of 'Tutorial' software in CAI?

CAI में 'ट्यूटोरियल' सॉफ्टवेयर की मुख्य विशेषता क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Tutorials are designed to teach new material directly to the student from start to finish, complete with information delivery and formative quizzes.

Q19. Under the SWAYAM platform, which quadrant focuses on 'Web Resources' (further reading, Wikipedia links, open source content)?

SWAYAM प्लेटफॉर्म के अंतर्गत, कौन-सा चतुर्थांश (Quadrant) 'वेब संसाधन' (अतिरिक्त पठन, लिंक) पर केंद्रित है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Quadrant 1: e-Tutorial (Video). Quadrant 2: e-Content (PDFs). Quadrant 3: Web Resources (links, further reading). Quadrant 4: Self-Assessment (Quizzes).

Q20. Which term refers to the disparity in access to digital tools, internet connectivity, and computer literacy among different socio-economic groups?

विभिन्न सामाजिक-आर्थिक समूहों के बीच डिजिटल उपकरणों, इंटरनेट और कंप्यूटर साक्षरता तक पहुंच में असमानता को क्या कहा जाता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The Digital Divide is a major challenge in implementing pure online education, as it highlights the gap between those who have ready access to computers/internet and those who do not.
