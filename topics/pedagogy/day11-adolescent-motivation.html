Day 11: Adolescent Psychology & Motivation

Storm & Stress, Erikson, Kohlberg, Maslow | Bilingual Notes

1. Adolescence & G. Stanley Hall (किशोरावस्था)

Adolescence (ages 11-18) is the transitional stage from childhood to adulthood. G. Stanley Hall (Father of Adolescent Psychology) famously coined the term "Storm and Stress" (तूफान और तनाव की अवस्था) to describe this period due to intense emotional upheavals, biological changes, and mood swings.

2. Erik Erikson's Psychosocial Theory (मनोसामाजिक विकास सिद्धांत)

Erikson proposed 8 stages of life. The 5th stage corresponds to adolescence:

Stage 5: Identity vs. Role Confusion (पहचान बनाम भूमिका भ्रम) (12 to 18 years)
• Key Question: "Who am I?" (मैं कौन हूँ?)
• Crisis: Teenagers explore independence, career choices, and values. Success leads to an 'Ego Identity' (knowing who they are). Failure leads to 'Role Confusion' (uncertainty about the future).

3. Lawrence Kohlberg's Moral Development (लॉरेंस कोहलबर्ग का नैतिक विकास)

Tested using moral dilemmas like the Heinz Dilemma. It has 3 Levels and 6 Stages:

  • Level 1: Pre-Conventional (पूर्व-रूढ़िगत): Morality is external.
    • Stage 1: Obedience & Punishment (दंड और आज्ञापालन).
    • Stage 2: Individualism & Exchange / Tit-for-Tat (जैसे को तैसा / आदान-प्रदान).
  • Level 2: Conventional (रूढ़िगत / परंपरागत): Morality is tied to society.
    • Stage 3: Good Boy-Good Girl (अच्छा लड़का-अच्छी लड़की).
    • Stage 4: Authority & Social Order (कानून और व्यवस्था बनाए रखना).
  • Level 3: Post-Conventional (उत्तर-रूढ़िगत): Morality is internalized.
    • Stage 5: Social Contract & Individual Rights (सामाजिक अनुबंध - Laws can change for the greater good).
    • Stage 6: Universal Ethical Principles (सार्वभौमिक नैतिक सिद्धांत - Conscious driven).

4. Motivation (अभिप्रेरणा)

  • Intrinsic Motivation (आंतरिक अभिप्रेरणा): Doing something because it is inherently interesting, enjoyable, or satisfies curiosity (e.g., painting for the joy of it). Highest form of motivation.
  • Extrinsic Motivation (बाह्य अभिप्रेरणा): Doing something to earn a reward or avoid punishment (e.g., studying just to get a bicycle from parents).

5. Abraham Maslow’s Hierarchy of Needs (मास्लो का पदानुक्रम)

Human needs are arranged in a pyramid. Lower needs must be met before higher needs.

  1. Physiological (शारीरिक): Food, water, sleep.
  2. Safety (सुरक्षा): Physical and emotional security.
  3. Love and Belongingness (प्रेम और संबंध): Friendship, family, peer groups.
  4. Esteem (सम्मान): Self-respect, recognition, status.
  5. Self-Actualization (आत्म-सिद्धि): Achieving one's full potential and creativity (Growth Need).

Q1. Who characterized adolescence as a period of "Storm and Stress" (तूफान और तनाव की अवस्था)?

किशोरावस्था को "तूफान और तनाव की अवस्था" के रूप में किसने चित्रित किया?

Explanation (व्याख्या): G. Stanley Hall is known as the father of adolescent psychology. He coined "Storm and Stress" in his 1904 book due to the biological and emotional turbulence of the teenage years.

Q2. According to Erik Erikson’s psychosocial stages, which core crisis do adolescents (ages 12–18) typically experience?

एरिक एरिक्सन के मनोसामाजिक विकास सिद्धांतों के अनुसार, किशोरावस्था (12–18 वर्ष) में कौन-सा संकट (crisis) होता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Adolescents are trying to figure out "Who am I?" If successful, they form a strong identity. If they fail to explore their options, they experience role confusion.

Q3. A student obeys school rules primarily to avoid getting punished by the teacher. According to Lawrence Kohlberg, this student is at which stage?

एक छात्र दंड से बचने के लिए नियमों का पालन करता है। कोहलबर्ग के अनुसार यह छात्र किस चरण पर है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Stage 1 (Pre-conventional) is purely about avoiding negative consequences (punishment).

Q4. A teenager behaves well simply to be regarded as a "good boy" or "good girl" by peers and teachers to gain social approval. This is:

एक किशोर समाज और सहपाठियों की नज़रों में "अच्छा लड़का" या "अच्छी लड़की" बनने के लिए अच्छा व्यवहार करता है। यह है:

Explanation (व्याख्या): The "Good Boy-Good Girl" orientation is Stage 3 (Conventional Level), where morality is driven by interpersonal relationships and the desire for social approval.

Q5. Which of the following is the classic example of 'Intrinsic Motivation' (आंतरिक अभिप्रेरणा)?

निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा 'आंतरिक अभिप्रेरणा' का सबसे अच्छा उदाहरण है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Intrinsic motivation comes from within—doing an activity for its own inherent joy, curiosity, and satisfaction, without any external reward.

Q6. In Abraham Maslow’s Hierarchy of Needs, which need occupies the absolute peak of the pyramid representing the realization of full potential?

अब्रहम मास्लो की आवश्यकताओं के पदानुक्रम में, पिरामिड का सर्वोच्च शिखर कौन-सी आवश्यकता दर्शाती है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Self-actualization is the peak of Maslow's pyramid. It is the desire to become everything one is capable of becoming (maximizing one's potential).

Q7. According to Maslow, physiological needs and safety needs are classified as:

मास्लो के अनुसार, शारीरिक आवश्यकताओं और सुरक्षा आवश्यकताओं को किस श्रेणी में रखा जाता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The first four levels (Physiological, Safety, Belonging, Esteem) are Deficiency Needs (D-needs). If they are unmet, a person feels anxious and tense. Self-actualization is a Growth Need (B-need).

Q8. What is the primary educational implication of Heinz’s Dilemma used by Kohlberg in his research?

कोहलबर्ग द्वारा उपयोग किए गए 'हेंज की दुविधा' (Heinz's Dilemma) का मुख्य शैक्षिक निहितार्थ क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Kohlberg was not interested in whether subjects said "yes" or "no" to Heinz stealing the drug; he analyzed the *reasoning* behind their answer to determine their stage of moral development.

Q9. Fostering 'Critical Thinking' in students primarily involves encouraging them to:

छात्रों में 'आलोचनात्मक चिंतन' (Critical Thinking) को बढ़ावा देने में क्या शामिल है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Critical thinking is the objective analysis and evaluation of an issue. It requires questioning assumptions, identifying biases, and logically testing evidence rather than accepting things at face value.

Q10. According to Kohlberg, at which stage do individuals view laws as flexible instruments that can be modified for the greatest good of society (e.g., amending the Constitution)?

कोहलबर्ग के अनुसार, किस चरण में व्यक्ति कानूनों को ऐसे लचीले साधनों के रूप में देखता है जिन्हें समाज के बड़े हित के लिए संशोधित किया जा सकता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): In Stage 5 (Post-conventional), individuals realize laws are social contracts. If a law is unjust or doesn't serve the greater good, it should be changed democratically.

Q11. A teacher who connects abstract scientific principles to real-world community problems and allows students to express their creativity is addressing which level of Maslow's hierarchy?

एक शिक्षक जो पाठ को वास्तविक दुनिया की समस्याओं से जोड़ता है और छात्रों की रचनात्मकता को बढ़ावा देता है, वह मास्लो के पदानुक्रम के किस स्तर को संबोधित कर रहा है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Creativity, problem-solving, and realizing one's inner potential correspond directly to Self-Actualization (the top of the pyramid).

Q12. The tendency of an external reward (like money or prizes) to decrease a person's intrinsic motivation to perform a task they already enjoy is known in psychology as:

किसी ऐसे कार्य के लिए बाह्य इनाम (जैसे पैसे) देना जिसे व्यक्ति पहले से ही पसंद करता है, जिससे उसकी आंतरिक अभिप्रेरणा कम हो जाती है। इसे मनोविज्ञान में क्या कहा जाता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The Overjustification Effect occurs when an expected external incentive decreases a person's intrinsic motivation to perform a task. (e.g. paying a child to read a book they already loved reading).

Q13. In the context of adolescent development, what does "Imaginary Audience" mean?

किशोर विकास के संदर्भ में, "काल्पनिक दर्शक" (Imaginary Audience) का क्या अर्थ है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Coined by David Elkind, Imaginary Audience is a form of adolescent egocentrism where teens feel they are constantly on a "stage", leading to high self-consciousness.

Q14. Which stage of Kohlberg's theory is often referred to as the "Tit-for-Tat" stage (I'll scratch your back if you scratch mine)?

कोहलबर्ग के सिद्धांत के किस चरण को अक्सर "जैसे को तैसा" (Tit-for-Tat) चरण कहा जाता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Stage 2 (Pre-conventional) is driven by self-interest and fair exchange. "If I share my lunch with you today, you must share yours tomorrow."

Q15. According to Erikson, if an adolescent fails to successfully resolve the "Identity vs. Role Confusion" crisis, they may experience:

एरिक्सन के अनुसार, यदि कोई किशोर "पहचान बनाम भूमिका भ्रम" संकट को सफलतापूर्वक हल करने में विफल रहता है, तो उसे क्या अनुभव हो सकता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Role confusion leaves adolescents unsure of their place in society, their career path, and their core values, making them drift without purpose.

Q16. Algorithmic / Logical Reasoning in computer science education relies heavily on:

कंप्यूटर विज्ञान शिक्षा में एल्गोरिदमिक / तार्किक तर्क मुख्य रूप से किस पर निर्भर करता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Algorithmic thinking (Computational Thinking) requires breaking a complex problem into smaller parts (decomposition) and designing a step-by-step sequential solution.

Q17. Carol Gilligan criticized Kohlberg’s theory of moral development primarily because:

कैरल गिलिगन ने कोहलबर्ग के नैतिक विकास के सिद्धांत की मुख्य रूप से आलोचना क्यों की?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Gilligan argued that Kohlberg's research sample was entirely male, leading to a bias that valued abstract justice (male norm) higher than interpersonal care and compassion (female norm).

Q18. According to Maslow, before a teacher can expect a student to actively engage in group activities and form friendships (Love & Belongingness), the school must first ensure:

मास्लो के अनुसार, इससे पहले कि शिक्षक छात्रों से समूह गतिविधियों (प्रेम और जुड़ाव) में शामिल होने की उम्मीद करे, स्कूल को पहले क्या सुनिश्चित करना चाहिए?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Maslow's hierarchy requires lower deficiency needs (food, safety, physical security) to be fulfilled before a person can focus on higher social and emotional needs.

Q19. A student who believes that their intelligence can be developed through hard work, good strategies, and input from others possesses a:

एक छात्र जो मानता है कि उसकी बुद्धिमत्ता (intelligence) को कड़ी मेहनत और रणनीतियों के माध्यम से विकसित किया जा सकता है, उसमें क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Coined by Carol Dweck, a 'Growth Mindset' is the belief that abilities are not innate or fixed, but can be improved with effort and perseverance.

Q20. When an adolescent refuses to wear a specific shirt because "everyone will laugh at me", this behavior is best explained by Elkind's concept of:

जब कोई किशोर किसी विशिष्ट शर्ट को पहनने से मना कर देता है क्योंकि "सब मुझ पर हँसेंगे", तो इस व्यवहार को किस अवधारणा द्वारा सबसे अच्छी तरह समझाया जा सकता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): This is the 'Imaginary Audience' effect. The teen assumes they are the center of everyone else's attention and hyper-focuses on perceived flaws.
