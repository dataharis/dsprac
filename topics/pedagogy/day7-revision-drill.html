Score: 0 / 50

Q1. Thorndike's law stating that connections are strengthened with practice and weakened when practice is discontinued is known as:

थार्नडाइक का वह नियम जो कहता है कि अभ्यास के साथ संबंध मजबूत होते हैं और अभ्यास बंद करने पर कमजोर होते हैं, क्या कहलाता है?

Explanation: Law of Exercise is divided into Law of Use (practice strengthens) and Law of Disuse (neglect weakens).

Q2. In a flipped classroom model, where do students typically engage with the lower-order thinking skills (like listening to the lecture)?

फ्लिप्ड क्लासरूम मॉडल में, छात्र आमतौर पर निम्न-स्तर के सोच कौशल (जैसे व्याख्यान सुनना) का अभ्यास कहाँ करते हैं?

Explanation: The Flipped Classroom reverses traditional teaching. Direct instruction (lower-order) is done at home via video, freeing up class time for interactive problem-solving (higher-order).

Q3. According to Vygotsky, what is 'Private Speech'?

वायगोत्स्की के अनुसार, 'निजी वाक्' (Private Speech) क्या है?

Explanation: Vygotsky argued that children talk to themselves (Private Speech) to guide their own behavior and solve problems.

Q4. Which of the following is NOT one of the 4 quadrants of the SWAYAM platform?

निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा SWAYAM प्लेटफॉर्म के 4 चतुर्थांशों (quadrants) में से एक नहीं है?

Explanation: SWAYAM is an online MOOC platform. Its 4 quadrants are Video, E-Content, Web Resources/Discussion, and Self-Assessment. Physical attendance is not required.

Q5. According to Piaget, a child who believes that the moving clouds are 'alive' and have intentions is displaying:

पियाजे के अनुसार, एक बच्चा जो मानता है कि चलते हुए बादल 'जीवित' हैं और उनके अपने इरादे हैं, वह क्या प्रदर्शित कर रहा है?

Explanation: Animism is a feature of the Pre-operational stage where children attribute human qualities (life, feelings) to inanimate objects.

Q6. Which educational initiative provides 'Energized Textbooks' with QR codes?

कौन-सी शैक्षिक पहल QR कोड वाली 'ऊर्जायुक्त पाठ्यपुस्तकें' (Energized Textbooks) प्रदान करती है?

Explanation: DIKSHA is the National Digital Infrastructure for Teachers that integrates QR codes in physical textbooks to link them to digital resources.

Q7. The maxim "Whole to Part" (पूर्ण से अंश की ओर) is based on which psychological school of thought?

"पूर्ण से अंश की ओर" शिक्षण सूत्र किस मनोवैज्ञानिक विचारधारा पर आधारित है?

Explanation: Gestalt psychologists (Wertheimer, Kohler, Koffka) believed that humans perceive the whole configuration before focusing on individual parts.

Q8. In B.F. Skinner's Operant Conditioning, removing an unpleasant stimulus to increase a desired behavior is called:

स्किनर के क्रियाप्रसूत अनुबंधन में, वांछित व्यवहार को बढ़ाने के लिए एक अप्रिय उद्दीपक को हटाना क्या कहलाता है?

Explanation: Negative reinforcement increases a behavior by taking away something annoying (e.g., taking an aspirin to remove a headache).

Q9. The 'Project Method' was developed by W.H. Kilpatrick. It is rooted in the philosophy of:

'प्रायोजना विधि' डब्ल्यू.एच. किलपैट्रिक द्वारा विकसित की गई थी। यह किस दर्शन पर आधारित है?

Explanation: The Project Method is the practical application of John Dewey's Pragmatism, focusing on "learning by doing" in a social environment.

Q10. In the 2001 Revised Bloom’s Taxonomy, the highest level of the cognitive domain is:

2001 के संशोधित ब्लूम वर्गीकरण में, संज्ञानात्मक क्षेत्र का सर्वोच्च स्तर क्या है?

Explanation: In 2001, Anderson & Krathwohl swapped Evaluation and Synthesis, renaming them to verbs. 'Create' is now at the apex.

Q11. According to Piaget, fitting new information into an existing schema is called _____, whereas altering the schema is called _____.

पियाजे के अनुसार, नई जानकारी को मौजूदा स्कीमा में फिट करना _____ कहलाता है, जबकि स्कीमा को बदलना _____ कहलाता है।

Explanation: Assimilation uses existing structures without change. Accommodation alters structures to fit new reality.

Q12. What is the total duration of a standard Micro-Teaching cycle?

एक मानक सूक्ष्म शिक्षण चक्र की कुल अवधि कितनी होती है?

Explanation: Micro-teaching cycle (Stanford model) takes 36 mins: Teach (6) > Feedback (6) > Replan (12) > Reteach (6) > Refeedback (6).

Q13. Which category in FIAC (Flanders Interaction Analysis) represents the teacher accepting and clarifying students' feelings?

FIAC में कौन-सी श्रेणी शिक्षक द्वारा छात्रों की भावनाओं को स्वीकार करने और स्पष्ट करने को दर्शाती है?

Explanation: Category 1 is "Accepts Feelings". It is part of indirect teacher influence.

Q14. In Dale's Cone of Experience, which of the following provides the most concrete (least abstract) learning experience?

डेल के अनुभव शंकु में, निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा सबसे मूर्त (सबसे कम अमूर्त) अधिगम अनुभव प्रदान करता है?

Explanation: Direct purposeful experiences sit at the absolute base of the cone, providing the most concrete, hands-on learning.

Q15. The Inductive Method of teaching proceeds from:

शिक्षण की आगमन विधि किस ओर बढ़ती है?

Explanation: Induction builds a rule by analyzing specific examples first.

Q16. The concept of 'Zone of Proximal Development' (ZPD) was introduced by:

'समीपस्थ विकास का क्षेत्र' (ZPD) की अवधारणा किसके द्वारा प्रस्तुत की गई थी?

Explanation: ZPD is a core concept in Vygotsky's Socio-Cultural theory.

Q17. Which of the following is an example of an Asynchronous ICT tool?

निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा एक अतुल्यकालिक (Asynchronous) ICT उपकरण का उदाहरण है?

Explanation: Asynchronous tools do not require real-time interaction. Students can engage at their own pace.

Q18. According to Piaget, 'Object Permanence' develops during which stage?

पियाजे के अनुसार, 'वस्तु स्थायित्व' का विकास किस अवस्था के दौरान होता है?

Explanation: Object permanence (knowing objects exist even when unseen) develops around 8-12 months in the Sensorimotor stage.

Q19. The Heuristic Method of teaching, developed by H.E. Armstrong, places the student in the role of a:

एच.ई. आर्मस्ट्रांग द्वारा विकसित ह्यूरिस्टिक विधि छात्र को किसकी भूमिका में रखती है?

Explanation: Heurisko means "I find out". The student independently discovers facts rather than being told.

Q20. In the 5E Instructional model, which phase is primarily used for assessment of learning?

5E शिक्षण मॉडल में, कौन-सा चरण मुख्य रूप से अधिगम के मूल्यांकन के लिए उपयोग किया जाता है?

Explanation: The 'Evaluate' phase allows the teacher to assess students' understanding and mastery of the concept.

Q21. Jerome Bruner’s 'Spiral Curriculum' is based on the idea that:

जेरोम ब्रूनर की 'सर्पिल पाठ्यचर्या' इस विचार पर आधारित है कि:

Explanation: Bruner argued that complex ideas can be introduced early and revisited with increasing depth (the spiral).

Q22. Classical Conditioning is associated with ________, while Operant Conditioning is associated with ________.

शास्त्रीय अनुबंधन ________ से जुड़ा है, जबकि क्रियाप्रसूत अनुबंधन ________ से जुड़ा है।

Explanation: Ivan Pavlov discovered Classical Conditioning (dogs/reflexes), and B.F. Skinner developed Operant Conditioning (consequences/reinforcement).

Q23. Which barrier of communication occurs when the vocabulary or symbols used by the teacher are not understood by the student?

संचार की वह कौन-सी बाधा है जब शिक्षक द्वारा उपयोग की जाने वाली शब्दावली या प्रतीकों को छात्र नहीं समझ पाते हैं?

Explanation: Semantic barriers relate to the meaning of words, language differences, or confusing jargon.

Q24. In Herbartian lesson planning, what is the purpose of the 'Presentation' step?

हर्बर्ट की पाठ योजना में 'प्रस्तुतिकरण' (Presentation) चरण का उद्देश्य क्या है?

Explanation: During presentation, the core content of the day's lesson is delivered to the students.

Q25. "Shaping" in behaviorism refers to:

व्यवहारवाद में "शेपिंग" (Shaping) का क्या अर्थ है?

Explanation: Shaping (Skinner) involves rewarding small steps that gradually lead to the final complex target behavior.

Q26. What does TPACK stand for?

TPACK का पूर्ण रूप क्या है?

Explanation: TPACK outlines the intersection of Technology, Pedagogy, and Content Knowledge required for effective digital teaching.

Q27. According to Piaget, a child who can arrange sticks from shortest to longest has achieved the skill of:

पियाजे के अनुसार, एक बच्चा जो छड़ियों को सबसे छोटी से सबसे लंबी तक व्यवस्थित कर सकता है, उसने कौन-सा कौशल प्राप्त कर लिया है?

Explanation: Seriation (Concrete Operational Stage) is the ability to sort objects according to a specific quantitative dimension like length or weight.

Q28. In CAI (Computer Assisted Instruction), which mode involves virtual replication of real-world scenarios, ideal for dangerous experiments?

CAI में, कौन-सा मोड वास्तविक दुनिया के परिदृश्यों का आभासी प्रतिरूपण है, जो खतरनाक प्रयोगों के लिए आदर्श है?

Explanation: Simulations allow learners to experiment safely in a computerized model of reality.

Q29. According to Krathwohl's Affective Domain, what is the highest level of emotional/value internalization?

क्राथवोहल के भावात्मक क्षेत्र के अनुसार, भावनात्मक/मूल्य आंतरिककरण का उच्चतम स्तर क्या है?

Explanation: Characterization is the peak of the affective domain, where a person's value system consistently dictates their behavior and worldview.

Q30. Reciprocal Teaching, based on Vygotsky's theories, uses four main strategies. Which of the following is NOT one of them?

वायगोत्स्की पर आधारित 'पारस्परिक शिक्षण' चार रणनीतियों का उपयोग करता है। इनमें से कौन-सा उनमें से एक नहीं है?

Explanation: The four strategies of Reciprocal Teaching are Summarizing, Questioning, Clarifying, and Predicting (SQCP). Memorizing is not included.

Q31. Which maxim of teaching is heavily aligned with Inductive reasoning?

कौन-सा शिक्षण सूत्र मुख्य रूप से आगमनात्मक तर्क (Inductive reasoning) के साथ संरेखित है?

Explanation: Inductive reasoning builds a general rule from specific (particular) examples.

Q32. In classical conditioning, 'Spontaneous Recovery' refers to:

शास्त्रीय अनुबंधन में, 'स्वतः पुनःप्राप्ति' (Spontaneous Recovery) का क्या अर्थ है?

Explanation: Even after extinction, if the subject is given a rest period, the conditioned response (salivation) may temporarily reappear when the bell is rung.

Q33. According to Simpson's Psychomotor Domain, what is the highest level (Level 7) involving the creation of new movement patterns?

सिम्पसन के मनोगत्यात्मक क्षेत्र के अनुसार, नया कौशल सृजन (Level 7) क्या कहलाता है?

Explanation: Origination is the highest psychomotor level, where the learner creates entirely new, highly skilled physical movements or performances.

Q34. In FIAC, Category 9 corresponds to:

FIAC में, श्रेणी 9 किससे संबंधित है?

Explanation: Category 8 is Pupil Response. Category 9 is Pupil Initiation, meaning the student volunteers an idea or asks a new question.

Q35. Brainstorming was primarily developed by Alex Osborn to foster:

एलेक्स ओसबोर्न द्वारा विकसित 'ब्रेनस्टॉर्मिंग' का मुख्य उद्देश्य क्या था?

Explanation: Brainstorming defers judgment to generate a massive quantity of creative ideas, encouraging divergent thinking.

Q36. Providing a student with temporary instructional support (like hints) within their ZPD is termed as:

छात्र को उसके ZPD के भीतर अस्थायी निर्देशनात्मक सहायता (जैसे संकेत) प्रदान करना क्या कहलाता है?

Explanation: Scaffolding is the temporary support from an MKO that is gradually faded as the learner becomes independent.

Q37. Which of the following is the longest and most critical step in Kilpatrick's Project Method?

किलपैट्रिक की प्रायोजना विधि में सबसे लंबा और सबसे महत्वपूर्ण चरण कौन-सा है?

Explanation: Executing the project is where the actual "learning by doing" takes place, taking up the majority of the time and effort.

Q38. "A student takes an aspirin to get rid of a headache." In behavioral terms, this is an example of:

"एक छात्र सिरदर्द से छुटकारा पाने के लिए एस्पिरिन लेता है।" व्यवहारिक शब्दों में, यह किसका उदाहरण है?

Explanation: The removal of the headache (aversive stimulus) reinforces the behavior of taking the pill.

Q39. According to Piaget, egocentrism (अहं-केंद्रितता) is a prominent characteristic of which stage?

पियाजे के अनुसार, 'अहं-केंद्रितता' किस अवस्था की प्रमुख विशेषता है?

Explanation: During the pre-operational stage (2-7 yrs), children struggle to see situations from another person's perspective.

Q40. The EDUSAT satellite (GSAT-3) is primarily associated with which form of education?

EDUSAT उपग्रह मुख्य रूप से किस प्रकार की शिक्षा से जुड़ा है?

Explanation: EDUSAT was launched by ISRO exclusively to support distance learning and tele-education in India.

Q41. A teacher using a physical model of a heart instead of a real human heart is using which level of Dale's Cone?

असली हृदय के बजाय हृदय के भौतिक मॉडल का उपयोग करना डेल के शंकु के किस स्तर पर आता है?

Explanation: Models, mock-ups, and simulations represent 'Contrived Experiences', which are just one step above direct purposeful experiences.

Q42. Which cognitive level is demonstrated when a student finds and fixes a bug in a computer program?

जब कोई छात्र कंप्यूटर प्रोग्राम में बग (त्रुटि) ढूँढकर ठीक करता है, तो यह किस संज्ञानात्मक स्तर को दर्शाता है?

Explanation: Debugging requires breaking down the code to find the logical flaw, which is Analysis.

Q43. Bruner's 'Enactive Mode' of representation corresponds most closely to which of Piaget's stages?

ब्रूनर का 'सक्रियता' (Enactive) मोड पियाजे की किस अवस्था से सबसे अधिक मेल खाता है?

Explanation: Both Enactive mode and Sensorimotor stage rely on physical, motor-based interactions with the environment to learn (0-2 years).

Q44. Which learning environment blends face-to-face instruction with online, digital instruction?

कौन-सा अधिगम परिवेश आमने-सामने के निर्देश को ऑनलाइन डिजिटल निर्देश के साथ मिलाता है?

Explanation: Blended learning combines the best of traditional classroom teaching with online digital platforms.

Q45. In problem-solving, what is the step immediately following "Analyzing and Collecting Data"?

समस्या-समाधान में "डेटा विश्लेषण" के तुरंत बाद का चरण क्या है?

Explanation: After understanding the problem and analyzing the facts, a scientist formulates a tentative hypothesis to test.

Q46. Under FIAC, 'Teacher Criticizing or Justifying Authority' falls under which category?

FIAC के तहत, 'शिक्षक का आलोचना करना या अधिकार जताना' किस श्रेणी में आता है?

Explanation: Category 7 records instances of teacher criticism or enforcing authority, which falls under 'Direct Teacher Influence'.

Q47. If a student's anxiety prevents them from understanding the teacher's lesson, the communication barrier is:

यदि किसी छात्र की चिंता उसे पाठ समझने से रोकती है, तो संचार बाधा है:

Explanation: Anxiety, fear, and emotional distress are psychological barriers in the receiver's mind.

Q48. In the 5E model, which phase occurs last to measure student mastery?

5E मॉडल में, छात्र की महारत को मापने के लिए सबसे अंत में कौन-सा चरण आता है?

Explanation: Evaluate is the final E, focusing on formative and summative assessment of the concepts learned.

Q49. Piaget stated that cognitive development relies on Biological Maturation, whereas Vygotsky stated it relies on:

पियाजे के अनुसार संज्ञानात्मक विकास जैविक परिपक्वता पर निर्भर है, जबकि वायगोत्स्की के अनुसार यह किस पर निर्भर है?

Explanation: Vygotsky's Socio-Cultural Theory emphasizes that cognitive growth is driven by social interaction and cultural tools like language.

Q50. The National Initiative for School Heads' and Teachers' Holistic Advancement is abbreviated as:

स्कूल प्रमुखों और शिक्षकों की समग्र उन्नति के लिए राष्ट्रीय पहल का संक्षिप्त नाम क्या है?

Explanation: NISHTHA is the flagship capacity-building and teacher training program launched by the Ministry of Education.
