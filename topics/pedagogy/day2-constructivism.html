Day 2: Cognitive & Social Constructivism (Piaget, Vygotsky & Bruner)

DSSSB TGT Section B | Detailed Bilingual Study Notes & Quiz

Constructivism (रचनावाद / निर्मितिवाद) posits that learners are not passive recipients of knowledge; rather, they actively construct their understanding of the world through experiences, social interactions, and cognitive reflection.

1. Jean Piaget’s Cognitive Constructivism (जीन पियाजे का संज्ञानात्मक विकास सिद्धांत)

Piaget viewed children as "Little Scientists" (नन्हे वैज्ञानिक) who explore their physical environment. According to Piaget, Biological Maturation and Development precede Learning (विकास, अधिगम से पहले आता है).

Four Adaptation Processes (4 अनुकूलन प्रक्रियाएं):

  • Schema (स्कीमा / मानसिक संरचना): Mental building blocks or organized structures of knowledge (मस्तिष्क में सूचनाओं के संगठित पैकेट या पूर्व-ज्ञान).
  • Assimilation (आत्मसात्करण / समावेशन): Fitting new information into an existing schema without modifying it (बिना किसी बदलाव के पूर्व-ज्ञान में नई जानकारी जोड़ना; जैसे—चार पैरों वाले कुत्ते को देखकर बिल्ली को भी कुत्ता समझ लेना).
  • Accommodation (समायोजन / संशोधन): Modifying an existing schema or creating a new one when new information does not fit (मौजूदा स्कीमा में बदलाव करना या नया स्कीमा बनाना; जैसे—यह समझना कि बिल्ली म्याऊं करती है और कुत्ता भौंकता है).
  • Equilibration (संतुलन / साम्यधारण): The cognitive balance between assimilation and accommodation. When faced with contradictions, children enter Cognitive Disequilibrium (संज्ञानात्मक असंतुलन / भ्रम), which motivates them to accommodate and reach higher understanding.

Piaget’s 4 Stages of Cognitive Development (पियाजे की 4 अवस्थाएं)

Stage & Age (अवस्था एवं आयु) Key Milestones (प्रमुख विशेषताएं) Cognitive Limitations / Exam Traps (सीमाएं)
1. Sensorimotor
(संवेदी-पेशीय)
0–2 Years		 Senses & motor reflexes; Object Permanence (वस्तु स्थायित्व) (~8–12 months: realizing hidden objects still exist). Lack of symbolic thought early on.
2. Pre-Operational
(पूर्व-संक्रियात्मक)
2–7 Years		 Symbolic thought, pretend play, rapid language development, transductive reasoning. Egocentrism, Animism, Centration, Irreversibility, Lack of Conservation.
3. Concrete Operational
(मूर्त संक्रियात्मक)
7–11 Years		 Conservation, Reversibility, Decentration, Seriation, Classification, Transitivity ($A > B, B > C \Rightarrow A > C$). Cannot think abstractly without concrete aids.
4. Formal Operational
(अमूर्त संक्रियात्मक)
11–15+ Years		 Hypothetico-Deductive Reasoning (परिकल्पनात्मक-निगमनात्मक तर्क), abstract logic, metacognition. Adolescent Egocentrism (Imaginary Audience & Personal Fable).

2. Lev Vygotsky’s Socio-Cultural Theory (लेव वायगोत्स्की का सामाजिक-सांस्कृतिक सिद्धांत)

Vygotsky argued that knowledge is co-constructed through Social Interaction, Culture, and Language. Unlike Piaget, Vygotsky maintained that Social Learning precedes and drives Cognitive Development (सामाजिक अधिगम विकास को गति देता है).

Three Pillars of Vygotsky (वायगोत्स्की के 3 मुख्य स्तंभ):

  • MKO (More Knowledgeable Other / अधिक जानकार अन्य): Anyone with higher proficiency in a target task—a teacher, peer, younger student, or computer/AI system.
  • ZPD (Zone of Proximal Development / समीपस्थ विकास का क्षेत्र): The gap between independent problem-solving (Actual Development Level) and potential problem-solving with guidance from an MKO.
  • Scaffolding (पाड़ / मचान / अस्थायी सहारा — Wood, Bruner & Ross): Temporary, adjustable support provided within ZPD and gradually withdrawn (fading) as the student achieves mastery.

Stages of Speech & Self-Talk (भाषा और स्व-भाषण के चरण):

  • Social Speech (0–2 yrs): External communication with others.
  • Private Speech (3–7 yrs): Speaking aloud to oneself for self-regulation, planning, and guiding behavior (स्व-नियमन हेतु खुद से बातें करना). (Exam Trap: Piaget called this "Egocentric Speech" and dismissed it as immaturity, whereas Vygotsky proved it is a vital self-regulation tool).
  • Inner Speech (7+ yrs): Private speech goes underground into silent internal thought.

3. Jerome Bruner’s Discovery Learning & Spiral Curriculum (जेरोम ब्रूनर)

  • Three Modes of Representation (प्रतिनिधित्व के 3 चरण):
    1. Enactive (0–1 yr): Action-based physical manipulation (शारीरिक क्रियाओं द्वारा सीखना).
    2. Iconic (1–6 yrs): Image-based visual diagrams and flowcharts (चित्रों व आरेखों द्वारा सीखना).
    3. Symbolic (7+ yrs): Abstract language, mathematical symbols, and code (अमूर्त भाषा व चिह्नों द्वारा सीखना).
  • Spiral Curriculum (सर्पिल पाठ्यचर्या): Fundamental ideas are taught early in simple form and revisited repeatedly across higher grades with increasing depth and abstraction.
High-Yield DSSSB Exam Summary (मुख्य परीक्षा बिंदु):
• Piaget: Independent explorer; Development precedes Learning; Egocentric speech.
• Vygotsky: Social co-constructor; Learning precedes Development; Private speech; ZPD & Scaffolding.
• Bruner: Discovery learning; Spiral curriculum; Enactive → Iconic → Symbolic modes.
