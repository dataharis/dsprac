Day 2: Constructivism (रचनावाद / निर्मितिवाद)

DSSSB TGT Section B | Piaget, Vygotsky & Bruner | Bilingual Notes

Constructivism (रचनावाद) is an approach to learning that holds that people actively construct or make their own knowledge and that reality is determined by the experiences of the learner. It shifts the focus from teacher-centered to learner-centered education.

1. Jean Piaget’s Cognitive Constructivism (जीन पियाजे का संज्ञानात्मक विकास सिद्धांत)

Piaget viewed children as "Little Scientists" (नन्हे वैज्ञानिक) who actively construct knowledge by exploring their environment. According to Piaget, Biological Maturation/Development precedes Learning (मानसिक विकास, अधिगम से पहले आता है).

Four Adaptation Processes (अनुकूलन की 4 प्रक्रियाएं):

  • Schema (स्कीमा): Mental building blocks or organized pockets of information in the brain (मस्तिष्क में सूचनाओं के संगठित पैकेट).
  • Assimilation (आत्मसात्करण): Fitting new information into an existing schema without modifying it (बिना किसी बदलाव के पूर्व-ज्ञान में नई जानकारी जोड़ना; उदा.- चार पैरों वाली बिल्ली को देखकर उसे भी कुत्ता समझना).
  • Accommodation (समायोजन / संशोधन): Modifying an existing schema or creating a new one when new information does not fit (मौजूदा स्कीमा में बदलाव करना; उदा.- यह समझना कि बिल्ली म्याऊं करती है, इसलिए वह कुत्ता नहीं है).
  • Equilibration (संतुलन): The cognitive balance between assimilation and accommodation. When faced with contradictions, children enter Cognitive Disequilibrium (संज्ञानात्मक असंतुलन), which drives them to learn.

Piaget’s 4 Stages of Cognitive Development (4 अवस्थाएं)

Stage & Age (अवस्था एवं आयु) Key Milestones (प्रमुख विशेषताएं) Cognitive Limitations (सीमाएं)
1. Sensorimotor
(संवेदी-पेशीय: 0–2 Yrs)		 Senses & motor reflexes; Object Permanence (वस्तु स्थायित्व). Lack of symbolic thought.
2. Pre-Operational
(पूर्व-संक्रियात्मक: 2–7 Yrs)		 Symbolic thought, pretend play, rapid language burst. Egocentrism (अहं-केंद्रितता), Animism (जीववाद), Centration, Irreversibility, Lack of Conservation.
3. Concrete Operational
(मूर्त संक्रियात्मक: 7–11 Yrs)		 Conservation (संरक्षण), Reversibility (उत्क्रमणीयता), Decentration, Seriation, Classification, Transitivity. Cannot think abstractly without physical/concrete aids.
4. Formal Operational
(औपचारिक संक्रियात्मक: 11–15+ Yrs)		 Hypothetico-Deductive Reasoning (परिकल्पनात्मक-निगमनात्मक तर्क), abstract logic, metacognition. Adolescent Egocentrism (Imaginary Audience & Personal Fable).

2. Lev Vygotsky’s Socio-Cultural Theory (लेव वायगोत्स्की का सामाजिक-सांस्कृतिक सिद्धांत)

Vygotsky argued that knowledge is co-constructed through Social Interaction, Culture, and Language. Unlike Piaget, Vygotsky maintained that Social Learning precedes and drives Cognitive Development (सामाजिक अधिगम विकास को आगे बढ़ाता है).

Three Pillars of Vygotsky (वायगोत्स्की के 3 मुख्य स्तंभ):

  • MKO (More Knowledgeable Other / अधिक जानकार अन्य): Anyone with higher proficiency in a target task—teacher, peer, or even an AI tool.
  • ZPD (Zone of Proximal Development / समीपस्थ विकास का क्षेत्र): The gap between independent problem-solving (Actual Level) and potential problem-solving with MKO guidance.
  • Scaffolding (पाड़ / मचान / अस्थायी सहारा): Temporary, adjustable support provided within ZPD and gradually withdrawn (fading) as mastery grows.

Stages of Speech (भाषा के चरण):

  • Social Speech (0–2 yrs): External communication with others.
  • Private Speech (3–7 yrs): Speaking aloud to oneself for self-regulation, planning, and guiding behavior (स्व-नियमन हेतु खुद से बातें करना). Note: Piaget called this "Egocentric Speech" (immaturity), whereas Vygotsky proved it is a vital cognitive tool.
  • Inner Speech (7+ yrs): Private speech goes underground into silent internal verbal thought.

3. Jerome Bruner’s Discovery Learning & Spiral Curriculum (जेरोम ब्रूनर)

  • Three Modes of Representation (प्रतिनिधित्व के 3 चरण):
    1. Enactive (सक्रियता: 0–1 yr): Action-based physical manipulation.
    2. Iconic (दृश्य/प्रतिमा: 1–6 yrs): Image-based visual diagrams and flowcharts.
    3. Symbolic (सांकेतिक: 7+ yrs): Abstract language, mathematical symbols, and code.
  • Spiral Curriculum (सर्पिल पाठ्यचर्या): Fundamental concepts are taught early in simple form and revisited repeatedly across higher grades with increasing depth and abstraction.

Q1. A student who already knows how to write a 'for loop' in C programming learns Python and modifies their mental understanding of loop syntax to use 'range()' and indentation instead of curly braces. According to Jean Piaget, this modification of an existing mental structure is called:

एक छात्र जो पहले से C प्रोग्रामिंग में 'for loop' लिखना जानता है, जब वह Python सीखता है तो कोष्ठक (curly braces) के बजाय 'range()' और इंडेंटेशन (indentation) का उपयोग करने के लिए अपनी मौजूदा मानसिक संरचना में बदलाव करता है। जीन पियाजे के अनुसार, पूर्व मानसिक संरचना में इस संशोधन को क्या कहा जाता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): When a learner modifies or restructures an existing schema (C loop syntax) to incorporate new rules (Python syntax), Piaget calls it Accommodation (समायोजन).

Q2. A 5-year-old girl talks aloud to herself while assembling a complex puzzle: "First I need the blue piece, no, that doesn't fit, let me try the red one." How would Piaget and Vygotsky respectively categorize this behavior?

एक 5 वर्षीय बच्ची पहेली को जोड़ते समय खुद से बोलकर बात करती है: "पहले मुझे नीला टुकड़ा चाहिए..."। जीन पियाजे और लेव वायगोत्स्की क्रमशः इस व्यवहार को क्या कहेंगे?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Piaget viewed self-talk as 'Egocentric Speech' (cognitive immaturity). Vygotsky proved it is 'Private Speech', an essential cognitive mechanism for self-guidance and planning.

Q3. In a Class 9 classroom, a teacher provides hints, flowcharts, and starter code to help students solve a difficult problem, and gradually removes these supports as students gain confidence. This instructional technique is known as:

शिक्षक छात्रों को समस्या हल करने में मदद करने के लिए संकेत (hints) देता है, और जैसे-जैसे छात्रों में आत्मविश्वास आता है, वह इस सहायता को धीरे-धीरे हटा लेता है। इस तकनीक को क्या कहते हैं?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Scaffolding refers to temporary, tailored instructional support provided within the ZPD that is gradually withdrawn ('fading') as the learner achieves independent mastery.

Q4. Which stage of Piaget's cognitive development is directly associated with 'Hypothetico-Deductive Reasoning' and the ability to systematically test multiple variables (such as in the Pendulum Problem)?

पियाजे की कौन-सी अवस्था 'परिकल्पनात्मक-निगमनात्मक तर्क' (Hypothetico-Deductive Reasoning) और कई चरों का व्यवस्थित परीक्षण करने की क्षमता से जुड़ी है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Formal Operational thought (ages 11–15+) enables adolescents to form abstract hypotheses, deduce logical consequences, and isolate variables systematically.

Q5. According to Lev Vygotsky, the 'Zone of Proximal Development' (ZPD) represents:

लेव वायगोत्स्की के अनुसार 'समीपस्थ विकास का क्षेत्र' (ZPD) किसे दर्शाता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): ZPD lies between the zone of actual development (independent performance) and the zone of potential development (assisted performance with an MKO).

Q6. A child is shown two identical glasses filled with equal amounts of water. When the water from one glass is poured into a taller, thinner glass, the child insists the taller glass now has 'more water'. According to Piaget, this failure to conserve volume is primarily due to:

बच्चा लंबे गिलास में 'ज़्यादा पानी' होने की ज़िद करता है। पियाजे के अनुसार आयतन के संरक्षण (Conservation) में यह विफलता मुख्य रूप से किसके कारण होती है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Pre-operational children exhibit Centration (focusing only on one dimension, like height) and Irreversibility (inability to mentally pour the water back).

Q7. Jerome Bruner proposed three modes of cognitive representation through which learners translate experience into a model of the world. What is their correct developmental order?

जेरोम ब्रूनर के संज्ञानात्मक प्रतिनिधित्व के तीन चरणों का सही विकासात्मक क्रम क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Bruner's modes progress from Enactive (action-based/physical manipulation) to Iconic (visual images/diagrams) to Symbolic (abstract language/symbols).

Q8. Which of the following classroom practices is a direct application of Vygotsky's Social Constructivism called 'Reciprocal Teaching'?

निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सी गतिविधि वायगोत्स्की के 'पारस्परिक शिक्षण' (Reciprocal Teaching) का प्रत्यक्ष उदाहरण है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Developed by Palincsar and Brown, Reciprocal Teaching uses four core comprehension strategies (Summarizing, Questioning, Clarifying, and Predicting) in a collaborative dialogue.

Q9. If a 9-year-old student understands that Stick A is longer than Stick B, and Stick B is longer than Stick C, and logically concludes that Stick A must be longer than Stick C, which Piagetian cognitive skill has the child mastered?

यदि A > B और B > C है, तो बच्चा निष्कर्ष निकालता है कि A > C होगा। बच्चे ने पियाजे के किस संज्ञानात्मक कौशल में महारत हासिल कर ली है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Mastered during the Concrete Operational Stage (7–11 yrs), Transitivity is the ability to recognize logical relationships among elements in a serial order.

Q10. What is the role of 'Cognitive Disequilibrium' (cognitive conflict) in a Constructivist classroom?

एक रचनावादी कक्षा में 'संज्ञानात्मक असंतुलन' (वैचारिक द्वंद्व) की क्या भूमिका होती है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Presenting students with a discrepant event or counter-example creates healthy cognitive conflict (disequilibrium), motivating them to accommodate and construct deeper understanding.

Q11. The concept of 'Spiral Curriculum'—where fundamental ideas of a discipline are taught early in simple form and revisited repeatedly across higher grades with increasing depth—was introduced by:

'सर्पिल पाठ्यचर्या' (Spiral Curriculum) की अवधारणा किसके द्वारा दी गई थी?

Explanation (व्याख्या): In his landmark book 'The Process of Education' (1960), Jerome Bruner introduced the Spiral Curriculum to gradually introduce and revisit complex concepts.

Q12. In an inclusive secondary computer lab, a teacher pairs a student struggling with database queries with a classmate who has already mastered SQL joins. Which Vygotskian concept does the skilled classmate represent?

एक कंप्यूटर लैब में निपुण छात्र जो किसी कमजोर छात्र की मदद कर रहा है, वायगोत्स्की के सिद्धांत के अनुसार किसका उदाहरण है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): An MKO does not have to be an adult or teacher; any peer, classmate, or interactive software system that possesses higher proficiency serves as the MKO.

Q13. According to David Elkind's extension of Piaget's Formal Operational stage, when a 14-year-old teenager refuses to go to school because of a tiny pimple, believing that "everyone will only be staring at my face", this is called:

जब एक किशोर चेहरे पर मुंहासे के कारण स्कूल जाने से मना कर देता है और सोचता है कि "सब मुझे ही देख रहे होंगे", तो इसे क्या कहा जाता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Adolescent Egocentrism has two components: Imaginary Audience (belief that everyone is watching/judging) and Personal Fable (belief in one's uniqueness and invincibility).

Q14. Which statement accurately captures the fundamental difference between Piaget and Vygotsky regarding the relationship between Learning and Development?

अधिगम और विकास के संबंध में पियाजे और वायगोत्स्की के बीच का मूलभूत अंतर क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Piaget maintained that brain maturation/development must happen first for learning to occur. Vygotsky argued that socially mediated learning (in the ZPD) pulls development forward.

Q15. In a Constructivist classroom (aligned with NCF 2005 and NEP 2020), how is the role of the teacher best defined?

एक रचनावादी कक्षा (NCF 2005 / NEP 2020) में शिक्षक की भूमिका को कैसे परिभाषित किया जाता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Constructivism shifts the teacher from a 'sage on the stage' to a 'guide on the side'—a facilitator who curates problem-solving environments and scaffolds student inquiry.

Q16. In Piaget's theory, the process of bringing information from the environment and fitting it into an already existing schema is called:

वातावरण से जानकारी लेकर उसे पहले से मौजूद स्कीमा में फिट करने की प्रक्रिया को क्या कहते हैं?

Explanation (व्याख्या): When new information is added to a schema without altering its core structure, it is Assimilation. Modifying the schema is Accommodation.

Q17. A child believes that the sun is following them when they walk. This feature of pre-operational thought is called:

एक बच्चा मानता है कि जब वह चलता है तो सूरज उसके पीछे चलता है। पूर्व-संक्रियात्मक सोच की यह विशेषता क्या कहलाती है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Animism (जीववाद) is the belief that inanimate objects (like the sun, toys, or clouds) have human-like feelings, intentions, and consciousness.

Q18. According to Vygotsky, what is the ultimate developmental trajectory of 'Private Speech' as children grow older?

वायगोत्स्की के अनुसार, जैसे-जैसे बच्चे बड़े होते हैं 'निजी वाक्' (Private Speech) का अंतिम विकासात्मक परिणाम क्या होता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Around age 7, external private speech internalizes into silent inner speech (thinking in words), which forms the basis of higher cognitive thought.

Q19. Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of Piaget's Concrete Operational Stage?

निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सी पियाजे की मूर्त संक्रियात्मक अवस्था की विशेषता नहीं है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Abstract Propositional Logic (अमूर्त तार्किक सोच) develops in the Formal Operational stage (11+ yrs). Concrete operational children require tangible, physical objects to reason.

Q20. According to Jerome Bruner, learning occurs through Discovery (खोज). Which type of reasoning forms the basis of Discovery Learning?

जेरोम ब्रूनर के अनुसार अन्वेषण अधिगम (Discovery Learning) का आधार किस प्रकार का तर्क है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Discovery learning relies on Inductive Reasoning (आगमनात्मक तर्क), where students explore specific examples to discover the general rule or underlying principle themselves.
