Day 4: Taxonomy & Lesson Planning

Bloom's Taxonomy, Domains, 5E Model & Micro-Teaching | Bilingual Notes

1. Bloom’s Taxonomy: 1956 (Original) vs. 2001 (Revised) (ब्लूम का वर्गीकरण)

Core Objective: Classification of educational learning objectives into hierarchical cognitive levels (शैक्षिक उद्देश्यों को संज्ञानात्मक स्तरों में वर्गीकृत करना).

Key Shifts (2001 by Anderson & Krathwohl):
1. Changed from Nouns to Verbs (संज्ञा से क्रिया में बदलाव).
2. Swapped the top two levels: Evaluation was the highest in 1956, whereas Create (रचना करना) became the highest level in 2001.

Level (स्तर) 1956 Original (Noun) 2001 Revised (Verb) Keywords (मुख्य क्रियाएं)
1 (Lowest) Knowledge (ज्ञान) Remember (स्मरण करना) Define, list, recall, memorize.
2 Comprehension (अवबोध) Understand (समझना) Explain, summarize, classify, illustrate.
3 Application (अनुप्रयोग) Apply (प्रयोग करना) Execute, solve, use, implement.
4 Analysis (विश्लेषण) Analyze (विश्लेषण करना) Differentiate, compare, debug, test.
5 Synthesis (संश्लेषण) Evaluate (मूल्यांकन करना) Critique, judge, defend, assess.
6 (Highest) Evaluation (मूल्यांकन) Create (सृजन करना) Design, construct, author, build.

2. Affective & Psychomotor Domains (भावात्मक एवं मनोगत्यात्मक डोमेन)

  • Affective Domain (Krathwohl, 1964): Focuses on emotions, values, and attitudes.
    Hierarchy: 1. Receiving (ग्रहण करना) → 2. Responding (अनुक्रिया करना) → 3. Valuing (मूल्यांकन करना) → 4. Organization (संगठन) → 5. Characterization (चरित्रिकरण).
  • Psychomotor Domain (Simpson, 1972): Focuses on physical coordination and motor skills.
    Hierarchy: 1. Perception (प्रत्यक्षीकरण) → 2. Set (तैयारी) → 3. Guided Response (निर्देशित प्रतिक्रिया) → 4. Mechanism (आदत/यांत्रिकी) → 5. Complex Overt Response → 6. Adaptation → 7. Origination (उद्भव/नया सृजन).

3. Instructional Models & Lesson Planning (शिक्षण मॉडल एवं सूक्ष्म शिक्षण)

  • 5E Instructional Model (Constructivist / BSCS):
    1. Engage (संलग्न करना): Hook students' interest, connect to prior knowledge.
    2. Explore (अन्वेषण करना): Hands-on inquiry and investigation.
    3. Explain (व्याख्या करना): Concepts introduced; teacher explains technical terms.
    4. Elaborate (विस्तार करना): Apply knowledge to new situations.
    5. Evaluate (मूल्यांकन करना): Assessment of student understanding.
  • Herbartian 5 Steps of Lesson Planning (हर्बर्ट के 5 पद):
    1. Introduction (प्रस्तावना) → 2. Presentation (प्रस्तुतिकरण) → 3. Comparison (तुलना) → 4. Generalization (सामान्यीकरण) → 5. Application (प्रयोग).
  • Micro-Teaching Cycle (सूक्ष्म शिक्षण चक्र - D.W. Allen, Stanford):
    Designed to train teachers in specific micro-skills. Total time = 36 minutes.
    Teach (6 min) → Feedback (6 min) → Re-plan (12 min) → Re-teach (6 min) → Re-feedback (6 min).

Q1. In the 2001 Revised Bloom’s Taxonomy by Anderson and Krathwohl, which cognitive level replaced 'Synthesis' at the apex (highest level) of the pyramid?

2001 के संशोधित ब्लूम वर्गीकरण में, पिरामिड के शीर्ष (सर्वोच्च स्तर) पर 'संश्लेषण' की जगह किस संज्ञानात्मक स्तर ने ली?

Explanation (व्याख्या): In 1956, 'Evaluation' was the highest. In 2001, Anderson and Krathwohl moved 'Evaluate' to level 5 and made 'Create' (formerly Synthesis) the highest level 6.

Q2. A Computer Science teacher asks students to write a complete Python script to automate a file-backup task. According to Revised Bloom’s Taxonomy, which cognitive level does this task target?

फ़ाइल-बैकअप कार्य को स्वचालित करने के लिए पूर्ण Python स्क्रिप्ट लिखना किस संज्ञानात्मक स्तर को लक्षित करता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Designing, building, or writing an entirely new script or project from scratch falls under the highest cognitive domain: Create.

Q3. Match the learning domains with their primary focus: I. Cognitive (Mental skills), II. Affective (Emotions/Values), III. Psychomotor (Physical skills).

सीखने के क्षेत्रों को उनके फोकस से सुमेलित करें: I. संज्ञानात्मक (मानसिक कौशल), II. भावात्मक (भावनाएं/मूल्य), III. मनोगत्यात्मक (शारीरिक कौशल)। कौन-से सही हैं?

Explanation (व्याख्या): This is the 3H Framework of education: Cognitive (Head), Affective (Heart), and Psychomotor (Hand). All three are correctly matched.

Q4. What is the correct hierarchical order of the lowest to highest levels in the 2001 Revised Bloom’s Taxonomy?

2001 के संशोधित ब्लूम वर्गीकरण में सबसे निचले स्तर से सर्वोच्च स्तर का सही क्रम क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The 2001 revision uses verbs and goes from bottom to top: Remember (1), Understand (2), Apply (3), Analyze (4), Evaluate (5), and Create (6).

Q5. Which of the following represents the highest level in David Krathwohl’s 'Affective Domain' hierarchy?

डेविड क्राथवोहल के 'भावात्मक क्षेत्र' पदानुक्रम में सर्वोच्च स्तर कौन-सा है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The stages of the Affective Domain are: Receiving, Responding, Valuing, Organization, and finally Characterization (acting consistently in accordance with internalized values).

Q6. In Elizabeth Simpson’s classification of the 'Psychomotor Domain', what is the initial foundational stage of skill acquisition?

एलिजाबेथ सिम्पसन के 'मनोगत्यात्मक क्षेत्र' वर्गीकरण में कौशल अर्जन का प्रारंभिक चरण कौन-सा है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Perception is the first level of the psychomotor domain, which involves using sensory cues to guide motor activity (e.g., estimating where a ball will land to catch it).

Q7. The '5E Instructional Model' (Engage, Explore, Explain, Elaborate, Evaluate) is primarily rooted in which educational philosophy?

'5E शिक्षण मॉडल' मुख्य रूप से किस शैक्षिक दर्शन पर आधारित है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The 5E model developed by BSCS is heavily rooted in Constructivism, where learners build their own understanding through experience and reflection.

Q8. What is the correct sequence of the 5 Herbartian Steps in traditional lesson planning?

पारंपरिक पाठ योजना में हर्बर्ट के 5 पदों का सही क्रम क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): J.F. Herbart's formal steps are: Introduction/Preparation, Presentation, Comparison/Association, Generalization, and Application.

Q9. What is the exact total time duration of a standard 'Micro-Teaching Cycle' developed by D.W. Allen at Stanford University?

स्टैनफोर्ड में डी.डब्ल्यू. एलन द्वारा विकसित 'सूक्ष्म शिक्षण चक्र' (Micro-Teaching Cycle) की कुल अवधि कितनी होती है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): A standard cycle takes 36 minutes: Teach (6m), Feedback (6m), Re-plan (12m), Re-teach (6m), and Re-feedback (6m).

Q10. In which phase of the 5E Instructional Model does the teacher formally introduce scientific terms and definitions after students have investigated the concept?

5E मॉडल के किस चरण में शिक्षक अन्वेषण के बाद वैज्ञानिक शब्दावली और परिभाषाओं का औपचारिक परिचय देता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): During the 'Explain' phase, learners articulate their ideas, and the teacher clarifies concepts, formally introducing vocabulary and definitions.

Q11. A multiple-choice question that asks students to identify the correct syntax of a Python function from four options belongs to which cognitive level of Bloom’s Taxonomy?

बहुविकल्पीय प्रश्न जो छात्रों से सही सिंटैक्स की पहचान करने के लिए कहता है, ब्लूम वर्गीकरण के किस स्तर से संबंधित है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Simply recalling or identifying factual knowledge (like syntax rules) requires only the lowest cognitive level: Remember (Knowledge).

Q12. Which cognitive level involves breaking down material into constituent parts, determining how parts relate to one another, and identifying underlying motives or fallacies (e.g., debugging code)?

सामग्री को छोटे भागों में तोड़ने और उनके आपसी संबंधों को समझने (जैसे कोड डीबग करना) से संबंधित स्तर कौन-सा है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Breaking a complex system into its components to see how they work together (like finding a bug in code) is Analysis (विश्लेषण).

Q13. In Micro-Teaching, what is the primary purpose of the 'Feedback' (पुनर्भरण) phase?

सूक्ष्म शिक्षण में 'फीडबैक' चरण का मुख्य उद्देश्य क्या होता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Feedback is diagnostic. It helps the trainee teacher identify what went well and what micro-skill needs refinement before they Re-plan.

Q14. A student judges the validity and logical consistency of two conflicting software design architectures based on given evaluation criteria. Which cognitive level is demonstrated?

दिए गए मानदंडों के आधार पर दो सॉफ्टवेयर डिज़ाइन की वैधता की समीक्षा (judge) करना किस स्तर को दर्शाता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Making judgments, critiquing, or assessing the value of ideas/materials against specific criteria is Evaluation (मूल्यांकन).

Q15. Which of the following is an example of an objective set in the 'Psychomotor Domain' for a Computer Lab practical session?

कंप्यूटर लैब प्रैक्टिकल के लिए 'मनोगत्यात्मक क्षेत्र' (Psychomotor Domain) का उद्देश्य कौन-सा है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Physical skills, motor coordination, and manual manipulation (like typing and assembling hardware) fall strictly under the Psychomotor Domain.

Q16. In the 5E model, which phase is explicitly designed to capture students' attention, activate prior knowledge, and stimulate curiosity?

5E मॉडल के किस चरण का उद्देश्य छात्रों का ध्यान आकर्षित करना और उनके पूर्व-ज्ञान को सक्रिय करना है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The Engage phase acts as a 'hook'—it raises questions and connects what students already know to the new concepts they are about to learn.

Q17. The Herbartian step where the teacher actually delivers the new learning material using various teaching aids is called:

हर्बर्ट का वह पद जहाँ शिक्षक विभिन्न सहायक सामग्रियों का उपयोग करके नई पाठ्य सामग्री प्रस्तुत करता है, क्या कहलाता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Presentation (प्रस्तुतिकरण) is the core stage where the actual subject matter is introduced to the students.

Q18. According to Anderson and Krathwohl's taxonomy, writing a summary of a chapter in your own words demonstrates which cognitive level?

अध्यायों का सारांश अपने शब्दों में लिखना किस संज्ञानात्मक स्तर को दर्शाता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Paraphrasing, summarizing, and translating information into one's own words indicates Comprehension/Understanding, as it goes beyond mere rote memorization.

Q19. Micro-teaching is a scaled-down teaching encounter. What is the typical number of students (usually peers) in a micro-teaching session?

सूक्ष्म शिक्षण एक सीमित शिक्षण प्रक्रिया है। इसमें आमतौर पर कितने छात्र (सहपाठी) होते हैं?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Micro-teaching scales down the complexities of normal teaching. Class size is reduced to 5-10 pupils, and lesson duration is reduced to 5-10 minutes per teach session.

Q20. In the Psychomotor domain, the stage where learned responses become habitual and movements can be performed with confidence and proficiency is called:

मनोगत्यात्मक क्षेत्र में वह अवस्था जहाँ सीखी गई प्रतिक्रियाएं आदत बन जाती हैं और गतियाँ आत्मविश्वास के साथ की जा सकती हैं, क्या कहलाती है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Mechanism is the intermediate stage in learning a complex skill where learned responses have become habitual and the movements can be performed with some confidence and proficiency.
