Day 12: Curriculum Design & Action Research

Spiral Curriculum, Hidden Curriculum, PAOR Cycle | Bilingual Notes

1. Curriculum: Etymology & Meaning (पाठ्यचर्या: शाब्दिक अर्थ एवं परिभाषा)

  • Etymology: Derived from the Latin word 'Currere', meaning **"Racecourse" (दौड़ का मैदान)**. Thus, curriculum is the path or course of experiences a student runs through to reach educational goals.
  • Cunningham’s Definition: "Curriculum is a tool in the hands of the artist (teacher) to mold his material (student) in accordance with his ideals in his studio (school)."
  • Curriculum vs. Syllabus:
    • *Syllabus* is a descriptive outline of topics covered in a specific subject (narrow scope).
    • *Curriculum* is the total planned experiences—co-curriculars, sports, values, and environment—provided by the school (broad scope). Syllabus is a subset of curriculum.

2. Types of Curriculum (पाठ्यचर्या के प्रकार)

  • Overt / Explicit Curriculum (स्पष्ट पाठ्यचर्या): The official, written document and prescribed syllabus taught openly in classrooms.
  • Hidden / Implicit Curriculum (छिपी हुई पाठ्यचर्या): Unspoken values, cultural norms, and behavioral expectations implicitly taught through school rules and teacher attitudes (Coined by Philip Jackson, 1968).
  • Null Curriculum (अव्यक्त / उपेक्षित पाठ्यचर्या): Topics, values, or perspectives that are intentionally or unintentionally left out of the school curriculum (Coined by Elliot Eisner, 1979).
  • Core Curriculum (कोर पाठ्यचर्या): Essential subjects mandatory for all students to ensure a common foundation of knowledge.

3. Curriculum Models: Tyler vs. Taba (पाठ्यचर्या मॉडल)

  • Ralph Tyler Model (1949): Deductive, Objectives-first, Top-Down model based on 4 fundamental questions: 1. Educational purposes/objectives, 2. Educational experiences, 3. Organization of experiences, 4. Evaluation.
  • Hilda Taba Model (1962): Inductive, Grassroots, Bottom-Up model. Argued that teachers should design curriculum. Consists of 7 steps starting with diagnosing student needs.

4. Action Research (क्रियात्मक अनुसंधान)

Action Research is conducted by teachers, principals, or school personnel to solve **immediate, practical, localized classroom problems**, unlike fundamental research which seeks universal laws.

  • Fathers: Kurt Lewin (coined the term in 1946) and Stephen M. Corey (applied it to education in 1953).
  • PAOR Cycle (पार चक्र):
    1. Plan (योजना): Identify problem and formulate action plan.
    2. Act (कार्यान्वयन): Implement the plan.
    3. Observe (अवलोकन): Collect data and evidence on results.
    4. Reflect (चिंतन/समीक्षा): Evaluate success and plan next iteration.

Q1. The word 'Curriculum' is derived from the Latin root 'Currere', which translates to:

'Curriculum' शब्द की उत्पत्ति लैटिन शब्द 'Currere' से हुई है, जिसका अर्थ क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): 'Currere' means racecourse. Curriculum is the track of experiences a learner runs through during schooling.

Q2. Who famously defined curriculum as "a tool in the hands of the artist (teacher) to mold his material (student) in accordance with his ideals in his studio (school)"?

किसने कहा था कि "पाठ्यचर्या कलाकार (शिक्षक) के हाथ में अपने आदर्शों के अनुसार अपने छात्र को ढालने का एक उपकरण है"?

Explanation (व्याख्या): This is C.D. Cunningham's famous artistic metaphor describing the role of curriculum and teacher.

Q3. What is the fundamental difference between 'Curriculum' and 'Syllabus'?

'पाठ्यचर्या' (Curriculum) और 'पाठ्यक्रम' (Syllabus) के बीच मूलभूत अंतर क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Syllabus is a narrow outline of topics for one subject. Curriculum is broad, covering all learning experiences, academic and co-curricular, provided by the institution.

Q4. The unwritten, implicit social rules, values, and cultural norms that students learn simply by attending school are referred to as the:

विद्यालय जाने मात्र से छात्र जो अनकहे सामाजिक नियम, मूल्य और सांस्कृतिक मानदंड सीखते हैं, उन्हें क्या कहा जाता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Philip Jackson (1968) coined the term 'Hidden Curriculum' to describe unspoken lessons, values, and routines conveyed in school environments.

Q5. Topics, perspectives, and skills that are intentionally or unintentionally omitted from the school educational experience constitute the:

वे विषय और दृष्टिकोण जिन्हें जानबूझकर या अनजाने में स्कूली शिक्षा से छोड़ दिया जाता है, क्या कहलाते हैं?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Elliot Eisner (1979) introduced the 'Null Curriculum' to highlight what schools do *not* teach, which is often as important as what they do teach.

Q6. Who proposed the top-down, deductive curriculum design model (1949) based on 4 fundamental questions (Objectives, Experiences, Organization, Evaluation)?

4 मौलिक प्रश्नों पर आधारित शीर्ष-से-नीचे (top-down) पाठ्यचर्या डिज़ाइन मॉडल (1949) किसने प्रस्तावित किया?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Ralph Tyler's objective-centered model (1949) is a deductive framework focusing on educational purposes, experiences, organization, and evaluation.

Q7. Hilda Taba advocated for an 'Inductive, Grassroots' curriculum model. What is its distinctive feature?

हिल्डा टायलर (Hilda Taba) के आगमनात्मक 'ग्रासरूट' मॉडल की मुख्य विशेषता क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Taba's 7-step model is a grassroots model where teachers participate actively in designing the curriculum starting with diagnosing student needs.

Q8. Who is credited with coining the term 'Action Research' in 1946, later applied to education by Stephen M. Corey?

1946 में 'क्रियात्मक अनुसंधान' (Action Research) शब्द गढ़ने का श्रेय किसे जाता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Kurt Lewin coined 'Action Research' in 1946 to describe research that deals with social action and solving immediate problems.

Q9. What is the correct sequence of the cyclical steps in Action Research (the PAOR cycle)?

क्रियात्मक अनुसंधान चक्र (PAOR चक्र) के चरणों का सही क्रम क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The standard Action Research cycle is Plan, Act, Observe, and Reflect, which then loops back into a revised plan.

Q10. What is the primary purpose of Action Research conducted by a school teacher?

एक स्कूल शिक्षक द्वारा किए जाने वाले क्रियात्मक अनुसंधान का मुख्य उद्देश्य क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Unlike fundamental/academic research that seeks broad generalizations, Action Research is localized, practical, and meant to solve immediate classroom issues.

Q11. Which principle of curriculum construction emphasizes that learning content must prepare students for future life challenges and emerging careers?

पाठ्यचर्या निर्माण का कौन-सा सिद्धांत इस बात पर जोर देता है कि सामग्री छात्रों को भविष्य के जीवन की चुनौतियों के लिए तैयार करे?

Explanation (व्याख्या): The forward-looking principle ensures that the curriculum is futuristic, equipping students with adaptable 21st-century skills.

Q12. The principle of 'Correlation' in curriculum design refers to:

पाठ्यचर्या डिज़ाइन में 'सहसंबंध' (Correlation) के सिद्धांत का क्या अर्थ है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Correlation prevents compartmentalization of knowledge by linking concepts across subjects (e.g., historical timelines taught alongside literature).

Q13. Jerome Bruner's 'Spiral Curriculum' is best described as:

जेरोम ब्रूनर के 'सर्पिल पाठ्यक्रम' (Spiral Curriculum) को सबसे अच्छे रूप में कैसे वर्णित किया जा सकता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Spiral curriculum revisits core ideas across grades, deepening student understanding at each pass in accordance with cognitive growth.

Q14. Which curriculum design centers around real-life problems and social issues rather than traditional academic subjects?

पारंपरिक विषयों के बजाय वास्तविक जीवन की समस्याओं और सामाजिक मुद्दों के इर्द-गिर्द कौन-सा पाठ्यक्रम डिज़ाइन केंद्रित है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Problem-centered curriculum focuses on social problems and real-world issues, empowering students to find practical solutions.

Q15. Who applied Action Research specifically to the field of education through his 1953 book "Action Research to Improve School Practices"?

अपनी 1953 की पुस्तक "Action Research to Improve School Practices" के माध्यम से शिक्षा के क्षेत्र में क्रियात्मक अनुसंधान किसने लागू किया?

Explanation (व्याख्या): While Kurt Lewin coined the term, Stephen M. Corey was the pioneer who introduced Action Research to school teachers and educational administration.

Q16. What is a major limitation of Action Research compared to fundamental academic research?

मौलिक अनुसंधान की तुलना में क्रियात्मक अनुसंधान की एक प्रमुख सीमा क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Because Action Research is tailored to solve a specific local problem in one classroom/school, its results cannot be easily generalized to all schools everywhere.

Q17. The principle of 'Flexibility' in curriculum construction ensures that:

पाठ्यचर्या निर्माण में 'लचीलेपन' (Flexibility) का सिद्धांत क्या सुनिश्चित करता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): A flexible curriculum avoids rigidity, allowing teachers to customize pacing, methods, and content to fit student diversity and local contexts.

Q18. In Ralph Tyler’s model of curriculum design, what is the very first step a curriculum planner must undertake?

राल्फ टायलर के पाठ्यचर्या डिज़ाइन मॉडल में, योजनाकार को सबसे पहला कदम क्या उठाना चाहिए?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Tyler's first question is: "What educational purposes should the school seek to attain?" Establishing clear objectives guides all subsequent steps.

Q19. Which type of curriculum design organizes content around separate, distinct academic disciplines (e.g., Physics, Chemistry, History taught independently)?

कौन-सा पाठ्यक्रम डिज़ाइन सामग्री को अलग-अलग शैक्षणिक विषयों (जैसे भौतिकी, रसायन विज्ञान) के इर्द-गिर्द व्यवस्थित करता है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): Subject-centered curriculum is the traditional approach where each subject stands alone with its own syllabus and periods.

Q20. In the PAOR cycle of Action Research, what is the primary activity performed during the 'Observe' phase?

क्रियात्मक अनुसंधान के PAOR चक्र में, 'अवलोकन' (Observe) चरण के दौरान मुख्य गतिविधि क्या है?

Explanation (व्याख्या): After acting on the plan, the researcher systematically observes and collects data (test scores, observation notes, surveys) to see how effective the intervention was.
