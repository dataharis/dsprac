1. Three-Schema Architecture & ER Diagram Symbols

Concept / ConstructMust-Know DSSSB Exam Rule
External (View) LevelHighest abstraction; customized user views (hides sensitive columns).
Conceptual (Logical) LevelOverall logical design (tables, attributes, constraints — WHAT is stored).
Internal (Physical) LevelLowest abstraction; disk blocks, B+ trees, byte offsets (HOW it is stored).
Logical vs Physical IndependencePhysical (change Internal without affecting Conceptual — easier); Logical (change Conceptual without affecting External — harder).
ER Symbols (Chen Notation)Strong Entity = [Rectangle] · Weak Entity = [[Double Rectangle]] (PYQ 2024) · Multivalued = ((Double Oval)) · Derived = (--Dashed Oval--) · Identifying Rel = <<Double Diamond>> · Total Participation = === Double Line.
Min Relational Tables for ER1:1 $\to$ 1 or 2 tables · 1:N $\to$ 2 tables (FK on N-side) · M:N $\to$ 3 tables (separate junction table) · Multivalued attr $\to$ +1 new table.

2. Keys, Integrity Constraints & FD Rules

ConceptDistinguishing Rule & Formula
Cardinality vs. DegreeCardinality = Rows (Tuples); Degree (Arity) = Columns (Attributes) (PYQ 2021 S3 Q.30).
Super / Candidate / Primary / Alternate$\text{CK} = \text{Minimal SK}$; $\text{Alternate Keys} = \text{CK} - \{\text{PK}\}$. If 1 CK of size $k$, $|\text{SK}| = 2^{n-k}$.
Entity vs. Referential IntegrityEntity Integrity: PK cannot be NULL. Referential Integrity: FK must match parent PK or be NULL (PYQ 2017, 2024).
Armstrong's Axioms & ClosurePrimary: Reflexivity, Augmentation, Transitivity. Attributes never on RHS are Essential (in every CK).

3. Normal Forms (1NF, 2NF, 3NF, BCNF, 4NF) Comparison

Normal FormExact Condition & Exam Shortcut
1NF (PYQ 2017, 2024)Atomic values only; disallows multivalued attributes, composite attributes & nested relations.
2NF (PYQ 2021)1NF + No Partial Dependency ($\text{Proper Subset of CK} \not\to \text{Non-Prime}$). Guaranteed if all CKs are 1-attribute!
3NF2NF + No Transitive Dependency. For $X \to A$: either $X$ is Super Key OR $A$ is Prime Attribute. Always Lossless & Dependency-Preserving.
BCNF (3.5NF)For EVERY non-trivial $X \to A$, $X$ MUST be a Super Key. Every 2-attribute table is in BCNF.
4NF & 5NF Supp.4NF: No non-trivial Multivalued Dependency ($X \twoheadrightarrow Y$). 5NF: Project-Join Normal Form.

4. Relational Algebra, Indexing, ACID & Concurrency

TopicMust-Know Formula / Exam Fact
6 Fundamental RA Ops$\sigma$ (Selection/rows), $\pi$ (Projection/cols, removes duplicates), $\cup$ (Union), $-$ (Set Diff — PYQ 2024), $\times$ (Cartesian: $|R|\cdot|S|$ rows, $d_1+d_2$ cols), $\rho$ (Rename).
Derived RA Ops$R \cap S = R - (R - S)$; Natural Join $R \bowtie S$; Division $R \div S$ ("FOR ALL" queries).
Indexing & ViewsPrimary: Ordered PK + Sparse (PYQ 2021, 2024) · Clustering: Ordered Non-Key · Secondary: Unordered + Dense · Views: ORDER BY disallowed in SQL:1999 CREATE VIEW (PYQ 2025).
ACID & ConcurrencyA & D: Recovery Mgr (Undo/Redo WAL) · I: Concurrency Mgr (2PL/Timestamps) · C: Programmer + Constraints · Conflict Serializable $\iff$ Precedence Graph has NO cycle.
