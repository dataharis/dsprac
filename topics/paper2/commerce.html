🔥 Top Repeated Exact Concepts in DSSSB TGT CS

  • Journal vs Ledger Roles: Journal = Book of Original Entry (chronological); Ledger = Principal Book of Accounts (analytical account-wise classification) — 01 Aug 2021 S2 Q.141 & 25 Jun 2023 S1 Q.34.
  • Depreciation Methods: Straight Line Method (SLM = Original Cost, constant annual charge, reaches zero) vs Written Down Value (WDV = Diminishing Balance, never reaches zero) — 01 Aug 2021 S2 Q.142.
  • Trading & P&L Numericals & Adjustments: Gross Profit (`Sales - COGS`), Fire Insurance Memorandum Trading A/c, and Unearned Income subtracted from P&L — 12 Sep 2025 S1 Q.61, Q.75, Q.76.
  • Elasticity & Utility Curves: Perfectly Elastic Demand (`Ed = ∞`, horizontal curve), Law of Diminishing Marginal Utility (`TU` max when `MU = 0`), Monotonicity of Indifference Curve, and U-shaped SMC curve — 01 Aug 2021 S2 Q.159, 07 Aug 2021 S3 Q.80, 25 Jun 2023 S1 Q.19, Q.76.
  • RBI Monetary Policy Tools: Quantitative (`Repo`, `CRR`, `SLR`, `OMO`) vs Qualitative/Selective (`Moral Suasion`, `Margin Requirement`) — 01 Aug 2021 S2 Q.160 & 07 Aug 2021 S3 Q.64.
  • Company Members & Management Principles: Public Ltd Company minimum 7 members (`Q.8`), Delegation vs Decentralisation (`Q.81`), and Bootstrapping (`Q.86`).
  • E-Commerce & FinTech: ANSI X.12 EDI standard (`Q.120`), Online Stock Trading Demat 5-step sequence (`Q.61`), Order Fulfilment (`Q.40`), and `'B'` = Business in B2B/B2C (`Q.73`).

📈 Evolution from 2021/2023 to 2025 (`56/24`)

2021 (`91/20`) & 2023 (`39/22`): Focused heavily on direct BCA definitions, sequence ordering (8-step Accounting Cycle, 5-step Online Stock Trading, E-Commerce Fulfilment), and two-statement True/False evaluations (Journal vs Ledger, SLM vs WDV, Quantitative vs Qualitative RBI tools).
2025 (`56/24` — 12 Sep 2025 Shift 1): Introduced 3 multi-figure Accounting Numericals & Scenarios out of 6 Commerce questions:
1. Q.61: Calculate Gross Profit (`₹40,000`) from Opening Stock (`₹25k`), Purchases (`₹1,40k`), Direct Wages (`₹15k`), Sales (`₹1,90k`), and Closing Stock (`₹30k`).
2. Q.75: Procedure to estimate stock destroyed by fire on 18 Oct 2024 (prepare Memorandum Trading A/c from 1 Apr to 18 Oct using historical GP rate).
3. Q.76: Rent received `₹2,40,000` for 12 months starting 1 Jun 2024 (year ends 31 Mar 2025) → only 10 months (`₹2,00,000`) credited to P&L; `₹40,000` is Unearned Rent (Liability).

2. Must-Memorise Accounting & Economics Formulas

  • Accounting Equation: Assets = Liabilities + Capital
  • COGS: Opening Stock + Net Purchases + Direct Expenses - Closing Stock
  • Gross Profit: Net Sales - COGS | Net Profit: GP + Other Income - Indirect Expenses
  • SLM Depreciation: (Cost + Installation - Scrap) / Useful Life
  • Fire Insurance Claim (Average Clause): Loss of Stock × (Policy Value / Stock on Date of Fire)
  • Price Elasticity (Ed): (-)(ΔQ / ΔP) × (P / Q)
  • Break-Even Point (Units): Fixed Cost / (Selling Price - Variable Cost per Unit)
  • National Income (NNP_FC): GDP_MP - Depreciation + NFIA - (Indirect Taxes - Subsidies)

4. RBI Monetary Tools & Digital Payment Rails

  • RBI Quantitative Tools (Q.64): Repo Rate, Reverse Repo, SDF, MSF, Bank Rate, CRR (Cash with RBI), SLR (Liquid Assets with self), OMO.
  • RBI Qualitative/Selective Tools (Q.160): Moral Suasion, Margin Requirements, Credit Rationing, Direct Action.
  • RTGS: Real-Time Gross Settlement | Min ₹2,00,000 | No Upper Limit.
  • NEFT: Deferred Net Settlement (Half-Hourly Batches) | No Min/Max Limit.
  • UPI: Instant 24×7 via VPA (NPCI) | Standard Limit ₹1 Lakh/day (₹5L for Healthcare/Education/IPO).
  • Online Stock Trading 5 Steps (Q.61): Select Broker → Open Demat A/c (DP) → Place Order → Match Price on Exchange → Contract Note & T+1 Settlement.
