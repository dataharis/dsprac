📐 Master Operating Systems Formula Sheet (TGT & PGT Numericals)

1. Turnaround & Waiting Time:
TAT = CT - AT  |  WT = TAT - BT  |  RT = First_CPU - AT
2. Deadlock-Free Minimum Resources (PGT 2021):
R_min = Σ(Max_i - 1) + 1 = n(k - 1) + 1
3. TLB Effective Access Time (EAT):
EAT = α(c + m) + (1 - α)(c + 2m)
4. Demand Paging EAT (Page Fault Rate p):
EAT = (1 - p) × m + p × Page_Fault_Service_Time
5. Logical Address & Page Table Size:
Pages = 2^(m - d)  |  Physical Addr = (f × 2^d) + d
6. Disk Access Time & Rotational Latency:
T_access = T_seek + (30,000 / RPM ms) + T_transfer
7. fork() Process Creation Count:
n fork() calls ⇒ Total = 2^n  |  New Children = 2^n - 1
8. Linux chmod Octal Weights (TGT 2017):
r = 4, w = 2, x = 1  |  -rwxr--r-- = 744  |  -rwxr-xr-x = 755
