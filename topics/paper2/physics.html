⚠️ Top 6 Electricity, Magnetism & AC Exam Traps

  • Stretching vs Cutting a Wire: Stretching to nL at constant volume makes R' = n²R (4× for double length), while resistivity ρ NEVER changes!
  • Bulb Brightness Trap: In Series (same I, P = I²R), the LOWER wattage bulb (higher R) glows brighter! In Parallel (same V), the HIGHER wattage bulb glows brighter.
  • Series Cells Terminal Voltage (2025 Q.73): Discharging terminal voltage is V = E_eq - I·r_eq; during charging it is V = E + Ir.
  • Capacitors vs Resistors: Capacitors in Parallel ADD (C_p = C₁ + C₂), whereas Capacitors in Series follow the reciprocal rule!
  • Stationary Charge in Magnetic Field: If v = 0 or θ = 0° / 180° (parallel/antiparallel to B), magnetic force F = qvB sinθ = 0. Magnetic force does ZERO work!
  • Transformer on DC Battery: A transformer works ONLY on AC (changing flux). On steady DC, dΦ/dt = 0 → V_s = 0 V (and primary winding may burn!).

⚠️ Top 6 Electronics, Modern Physics & SI Unit Traps

  • N-Type & P-Type Charge Neutrality: Both N-type and P-type semiconductors are 100% electrically neutral overall!
  • Temperature Effect on R: Heating a metal increases R (positive α), whereas heating a semiconductor exponentially DECREASES R (negative α)!
  • Photoelectric Intensity vs Frequency (2023 Q.52): Maximum KE (K_max = hf - φ₀) depends ONLY on frequency, never intensity. Increasing intensity increases photocurrent only (if f > f₀).
  • Memory Physics (2023 Q.49 & 2025 Q.84): DRAM stores bits as charge on a Capacitor (needs refresh); EPROM is erased by UV Light; EEPROM/Flash is erased electrically.
  • Heat Units across Systems (2023 Q.69): CGS = Calorie (4.184 J), FPS = BTU (1055 J), SI = Joule (J).
  • Sub-Multiple Prefixes (2021 Q.105): nano = 10⁻⁹, pico = 10⁻¹², femto = 10⁻¹⁵, atto = 10⁻¹⁸, 1 Å = 10⁻¹⁰ m.
