🔍 Crucial Syllabus Verification: DoE vs NDMC Stream
Syllabus Proof

Is Basics of Physics officially included in DSSSB TGT Computer Science? YES — with a critical stream distinction:

  • Directorate of Education (DoE) Stream (`Post Codes 192/14, 91/20, 39/22, 56/24, 41/26`): Follows the 32-Unit BCA-level syllabus where Unit 6: Basis of Physics and Unit 4: Digital Electronics are explicitly prescribed! Direct numericals on Cell Terminal Voltage (`V = E - Ir`, Sep 2025 Q.73), Bar Magnet & Solenoid Equivalence (Sep 2025 Q.79), Photoelectric Effect (Jun 2023 Q.52), Heat Units CGS/FPS/SI (Jun 2023 Q.69), Electric Power (`P = VI`, Aug 2021 Q.28), Series Resistors (`3×3Ω = 9Ω`, Aug 2021 Q.125), and Vector Calculus (`∇·(∇×P⃗)=0`, Aug 2021 Q.20) appear in every cycle.
  • NDMC Stream (`Post Code 70/23`, Exam Feb 2024): Uses the 13-Subject syllabus focusing on core CS, where pure Physics is reduced to Wave Propagation Physics (`λ = v × T`, Feb 2024 Q.83; Sine Wave Time-Domain Trap, Q.84) and Digital Logic Gates & Semiconductor Memory (`DRAM Capacitor, EPROM UV`).

❌ Skip Heavy Classical Mechanics & Gravitation:

Projectile motion on inclined planes, Moment of Inertia integrations, Rolling motion without slipping, Kepler's orbital mechanics derivations, and Bernoulli's fluid viscosity equations have 0 questions in DSSSB TGT Computer Science (2014–2025).

❌ Skip Thermodynamic Engines & Ray Optics Lens Maker Derivations:

Carnot cycle PV-diagram integrations and thick-lens aberration derivations are NOT asked. Only study Units of Heat (Calorie, BTU, Joule — 2023 Q.69), Temperature scales (°C, °F, K), and Optical Fiber Total Internal Reflection (TIR).
