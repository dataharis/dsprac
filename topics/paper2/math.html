📌 Exam Priority Architecture (90% TGT : 10% PGT)

In DSSSB TGT Computer Science (DoE Post Codes 91/20, 39/22, and 56/24), Engineering Mathematics contributes 5 to 8 questions per 100-question technical paper. Unlike GATE or PGT exams that test lengthy multi-page derivations, DSSSB TGT tests fast 45-second numerical & formula recognition:

  • Calculus (90% TGT Core): Standard limits ($\lim \frac{\sin ax}{bx}$), L'Hôpital's rule, cubic maxima/minima, definite integral symmetry ($\int_0^{\pi/2}$), Cauchy's Integral Formula ($\oint \frac{e^z}{z+2}dz = 2\pi i e^{-2}$), and $\nabla\cdot(\nabla\times\vec{P}) = 0$.
  • Differential Equations (90% TGT Core): Order/degree/linearity of ODEs & PDEs, forming ODEs by eliminating arbitrary constants, Integrating Factor ($I.F. = e^{\int P dx}$), and 2nd-order auxiliary roots.
  • Probability (90% TGT Core): Complementary independent hit probability ($1 - \prod(1-p_i)$), Bayes' Theorem (2-bag red ball & 3-factory defective bulb), Binomial ($np, npq$), and Poisson ($P(X=1)=P(X=2)$).
  • Statistics (90% TGT Core): Mean, Median with unknown $x$, Median shift ($3P-2Q$), Karl Pearson's $\text{Mode} = 3\text{Median} - 2\text{Mean}$, Range ($L-S$), Regression slope $b_{yx}$, $r = \pm\sqrt{b_{yx}b_{xy}$, and $\chi^2$ $2\times 2$ Independence Test (`2025 DoE Q.82`).
  • Interpolation & Numerical Methods (90% TGT Core + 10% PGT): Lagrange's polynomial for unequal intervals (`2021 Q.83`, `2023 Q.43`), Newton's Forward/Backward difference tables, Trapezoidal vs Simpson's 1/3 & 3/8 rules, Newton-Raphson order 2, and Matrix Eigenvalues/Rank.

⚡ Top 10 DSSSB TGT Mathematics Exam Traps to Avoid

  1. Order vs Degree of PDE/ODE: Always clear fractional exponents like $[1+(y')^2]^{3/2} = y''$ by squaring first ($\text{Order}=2, \text{Degree}=2$). Never confuse order with degree (`01 Aug 2021 S2 Q.101`).
  2. Cauchy Contour Pole Check: In $\oint_{|z|=R} \frac{f(z)}{z-a}dz$, first check if $|a| < R$. If $|a| > R$, the integral is immediately $0$; if $|a| < R$, it equals $2\pi i f(a)$ (`01 Aug 2021 S2 Q.102`).
  3. Independent Target Hits: For "at least one hits", multiply the miss probabilities and subtract from 1: $1 - \prod(1-p_i)$ (`07 Aug 2021 S3 Q.1`).
  4. Median Sorting Rule: Always sort observations in ascending order before finding the median or solving for an unknown $x+1$ (`01 Aug 2021 S2 Q.189`).
  5. Karl Pearson Empirical Mode: Memorize $\text{Mode} = 3\text{Median} - 2\text{Mean}$ (alphabetical distance ratio $1:2$).
  6. Regression Sign Consistency: Both $b_{yx}$ and $b_{xy}$ must have the same sign, and $r = \pm\sqrt{b_{yx}\cdot b_{xy}$ shares that sign.
  7. Variance Under Linear Shift $Y = aX + b$: Adding $+b$ does NOT change SD or Variance: $\sigma_Y = |a|\sigma_X$ and $\text{Var}(Y) = a^2\text{Var}(X)$.
  8. Chi-Square $2\times 2$ Degrees of Freedom: $\text{df} = (r-1)(c-1) = 1$, and $E_{ij} = \frac{R_i \times C_j}{N}$ (`2025 DoE Q.82`).
  9. Unequal vs Equal Interval Interpolation: Use Lagrange's Formula or Newton's Divided Difference for unequal intervals (`2023 Q.43`), and Newton's Forward/Backward for equal intervals.
  10. Simpson's 1/3 vs 3/8 Subintervals: Simpson's 1/3 requires $n$ to be even (exact up to cubics, degree 3!), while Simpson's 3/8 requires $n$ to be a multiple of 3.
