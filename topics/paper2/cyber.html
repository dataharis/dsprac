1–4. CIA Triad, Malware Types, Cyberattacks & Firewall/IDS/IPS

  • • CIA Triad: Confidentiality = Secrecy via Encryption (AES/RSA); Integrity = Accuracy via Hashing (SHA-256) & Digital Signatures; Availability = Uptime via RAID (2017 Q.142), Backups & DDoS defense.
  • • Malware Matrix: Virus (Needs Host File + Self-Replicates); Worm (Standalone + Self-Replicates over Networks); Trojan Horse (Fake Benign Utility + No Self-Replication + Backdoor); Ransomware (Encrypts files for ransom); Rootkit (Kernel stealth).
  • • Attacks: Phishing (Email), Spear Phishing (Targeted), Whaling (CEO), Vishing (Voice), Smishing (SMS). SQLi (' OR 1=1 -- $\to$ Stopped by Prepared Statements); XSS (Stopped by Output Encoding + HttpOnly cookie); CSRF (Stopped by Anti-CSRF Token + SameSite).
  • • Network Defense: IDS = Passive Detective Alert; IPS = Inline Preventive Drop. Wi-Fi: WEP (Broken RC4) < WPA (TKIP) < WPA2 (AES-CCMP) < WPA3 (SAE).

5–8. Symmetric vs Asymmetric, DES/3DES/AES, RSA & Hashing vs Encryption

  • • Key Counts ($n$ Users): Symmetric $= \frac{n(n-1)}{2}$ keys; Asymmetric $= 2n$ keys.
  • • DES vs 3DES vs AES: DES = 64-bit block, 56-bit key, 16 Feistel rounds (Obsolete). 3DES (PYQ 2023 Q.70) = 64-bit block, 168-bit (3 keys) / 112-bit (2 keys). AES = 128-bit block, 128/192/256-bit key (10/12/14 rounds).
  • • RSA (PYQ 2025 Q.6): $n = pq$, $\phi(n) = (p-1)(q-1)$, $ed \equiv 1 \pmod{\phi(n)}$. Confidentiality: Encrypt with Recipient's Public Key $\to$ Decrypt with Recipient's Private Key.
  • • Hashing (One-Way Integrity): MD5 = 128 bits (Broken), SHA-1 = 160 bits (Deprecated), SHA-256 = 256 bits (64 Hex chars — Standard). Salt defeats Rainbow Tables.

9–12. Digital Signatures, PKI/TLS & Indian IT Act, 2000

  • • Digital Signature: Sign hash with Sender's Private Key $\to$ Verify with Sender's Public Key (Provides Authentication + Integrity + Non-Repudiation).
  • • PKI & X.509 Certificate (PYQ 2021 Q.170 & 2025 Q.15): Issued by a trusted Certificate Authority (CA) to bind an entity's identity to its Public Key. HTTPS = Port 443 over TLS.
  • • IT Act, 2000 (Enforced 17 Oct 2000; Amended 2008): Sec 3 (Digital Signatures), Sec 3A (Electronic Signatures), Sec 4 (Electronic Records), Sec 43 & 66 (Hacking — 3 yrs / Rs 5L), Sec 65 (Source Code Tampering), Sec 66A (STRUCK DOWN by SC in Shreya Singhal 2015!).
  • • Key Offence Sections: Sec 66B (Stolen device), Sec 66C (Identity Theft), Sec 66D (Cheating by Personation / Phishing), Sec 66E (Privacy Violation), Sec 66F (Cyber Terrorism — Life Imprisonment), Sec 67/67A/67B (Obscene/CSAM), Sec 70B (CERT-In), Sec 79 (Intermediary Safe Harbour).

13–16. DPDP Act 2023, IPR, E-Waste Rules & Cyber Safety Tips

  • • DPDP Act, 2023 (Act 22 of 2023 — Assented 11 Aug 2023): Child = Individual under 18 years of age (PYQ 2024 Q.31); requires verifiable parental consent and bans child tracking/targeted ads (Sec 9). Max penalty = Rs 250 Crore. Replaces Sec 43A of IT Act.
  • • IPR & Licensing (PYQ 2024 & 2025): Software source code = Copyright Act, 1957; Inventions = Patent (20 yrs); Logos = Trademark (10-yr renewal). FOSS (GIMP, Linux — GNU GPL Copyleft) vs Freeware (closed source).
  • • E-Waste Rules (PYQ 2024 Q.47 & Q.49): Covers IT/Telecom & Consumer Electricals; enforces Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) & 3Rs (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle).
  • • Cyber Safety & Reporting: UPI PIN is NEVER needed to receive money. Report cyber financial fraud immediately on toll-free helpline 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in.
