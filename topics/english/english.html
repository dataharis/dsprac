Verified Official English Syllabus Wording

Section A — English Language & Comprehension:
"Testing of candidate's understanding and comprehension of the English Language and in addition to this, questions on its Vocabulary, Grammar, Sentence Structure, Synonyms, Antonyms and its correct usage etc. would also be covered."
— Verified from DSSSB One-Tier Examination Scheme (Official Notification Text & Post Code 56/24 Syllabus Audit).

Does English have a separate syllabus PDF? No—the 20-question English test in Section A follows DSSSB's common Section A syllabus above. Interestingly, the 32-item Section B syllabus PDF for Post Code 56/24 (pc_56-24.pdf) also lists Item 2: "Business Communication, Business Organization and Management, Writing" and Item 28: "Foundation Course in English" as part of its degree-level subject list.

Transparency Log: Unverified Details & Discrepancies

  • Separate English Section Timer: Not verified from the applicable official document. Official notifications prescribe a composite 120-minute (2-hour) duration for all 200 questions without a separate official timer for the 20 English questions alone.
  • Topic-Wise Question Count Breakdown Within English: Not verified from the applicable official document. DSSSB does not publish a fixed sub-topic blueprint (e.g., exact number of idiom vs. spelling questions); counts vary slightly by shift.
  • Section A Minimum Qualifying Marks Discrepancy: Some coaching blogs claim a 40% minimum qualifying cutoff applies separately to Section A. However, DSSSB's official One-Tier Technical notice (corroborated by the 27 Feb 2024 notice for Post Code 39/22, KnowledgeGate Audit) clarifies that mandatory minimum qualifying marks apply to Section B, while both Section A and Section B marks combine for final merit.
  • Automated Bot Crawl Limitation: Direct automated crawling of dsssb.delhi.gov.in URLs returned a firewall block on 27 Oct 2026; official notification texts and CBT response sheets were verified via verbatim archival PDFs linked in Section 10.
