Phase 1 Research: Key DSSSB Traps
  • 1. "8 Times More Than" Linguistic Trap (TGT CS 2024 Q.2): In DSSSB official keys, "E1 is 8 times more than E2" means E1 = E2 + 8·E2 = 9·E2, giving (9-1)/9 = 88.88% (not 7/8 = 87.5%).
  • 2. Cuboid TSA is 2D, Not 3D (TGT CS 2024 Q.7): Decreasing l, b, h of a cuboid by 12% reduces Total Surface Area by 12 + 12 - 1.44 = 22.56% because TSA = 2(lb+bh+hl) is degree-2!
  • 3. Decimal-Shifted Cubic Identities (TGT/PRT PYQ): Expressions like [(0.86)³+(0.14)³]/[(8.6)²+(1.4)²-86×0.14] always cancel to (a+b)/10² = 0.01.
