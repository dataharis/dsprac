Evidence-Based Exam Findings

1. Static GK vs. Current Affairs Split

Across the 10 complete TGT CS Section-A papers (200 questions), 84.0% (168/200) test permanent Static/Core GK (History, Polity, Geography, Science, Economy, Classical Culture, Delhi Heritage) and 16.0% (32/200) test Current Affairs from the 6–10 months immediately preceding the exam date.

2. Direct NCERT Line-by-Line Extraction

In the 2021, 2023, and 2024 TCS iON (Digialm) CBT papers, multi-statement ("Only I", "Both I and II") and single-liner questions are lifted word-for-word from NCERT Class 6–10 Social Science & Science and NCERT Class 11–12 Macroeconomics & Art/Culture (e.g., Natya Shastra 4-fold instruments, Bangar/Khadar soil, Coniferous trees, Spherical mirrors, and Revenue vs. Capital receipts).

3. Delhi GK & Computer Awareness Weightage

Delhi GK accounts for 1 to 3 questions per TGT CS shift (6.3% of TGT CS GA), focusing on Delhi Sultanate monuments (Sultan Ghari, Qutb Minar, Lodi dynasty), Mughal tombs, Article 239AA / 69th Amendment, LG of Delhi, and MCD elections. Computer/Tech Awareness in Section A (1–2 Qs in 2023–2024) tests 5G rollout, IMC New Delhi, ISRO-Microsoft MoU, and Bharat GPT.

4. High Adjacent-Shift Repetition

Candidates appearing in later shifts of the same recruitment cycle see 2 to 4 direct concept repeats per shift from earlier shifts (e.g., Kanya Shiksha Pravesh Utsav asked on both 24 June 2023 S3 and 25 June 2023 S3; Battle of Khanwa 1527 in both S2 and S3 on 24 June 2023; Natya Shastra instruments in 4 shifts of Aug 2021).

1. Frequently Tested Constitutional Articles & Amendments (Polity PYQs)

  • • Article 17 & 18: Abolition of Untouchability (Art 17 — 2019 PRT) & Abolition of Titles except military/academic (Art 18 — 2017 TGT CS Q.9).
  • • Article 19(2) & 1st Amendment (1951): Added 'public order', 'friendly relations with foreign states', and 'incitement to an offence' as restrictions on Freedom of Speech (2023 J25S3 Q.13).
  • • Article 21: Protection of Life and Personal Liberty; cannot be suspended even during Emergency under Art 359 (2024 F7S2 Q.8).
  • • Article 51A (Part IVA — Fundamental Duties): 10 Duties added by 42nd Amendment (1976) on Swaran Singh Committee recommendation (2025 PGT Eng Q.2); Art 51A(i) = Safeguard public property & abjure violence (2025 TGT San S2 Q.1); 11th Duty [Art 51A(k)] = Education for child 6–14 yrs added by 86th Amendment (2002) (2025 TGT San S1 Q.2).
  • • Article 61 & 65: Impeachment of President (Art 61 — 2021 A7S2 Q.9); Vice-President acting as President during vacancy (Art 65 — 2024 F7S2 Q.18).
  • • Article 112: Annual Financial Statement (Union Budget — 2021 A7S3 Q.6).
  • • Article 123 vs. Article 213: Ordinance making power of President (Art 123) vs. Governor of a State (Art 213 — 2023 J25S3 Q.11).
  • • Article 148: Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) of India (2024 PGT PE Q.16).
  • • Article 153 & 7th Amendment (1956): Common Governor for two or more states (2023 J24S3 Q.2).
  • • Article 239AA & 69th Amendment (1991): Special status of NCT of Delhi, Legislative Assembly (70 seats), 10% Cabinet cap (7 Ministers), and LG of Delhi (2021 A1S2 Q.10, 2021 A7S3 Q.5, 2023 J24S3 Q.8).
  • • Panchayats & Municipalities (Part IX & IX-A): Art 243D = Reservation of seats in Panchayats (2025 TGT SST); Art 243E = 5-year tenure (2025 PGT Eng); Art 243K / 243ZA = State Election Commission for Panchayat & Municipal elections (2021 A1S2 Q.20).
  • • Article 275, 315 & 343: Statutory Grants-in-aid to States (Art 275 — 2023 J25S2 Q.6); UPSC & State PSCs (Art 315 — 2024 F7S2 Q.6); Hindi in Devanagari script as Official Language of Union (Art 343 — 2024 F7S2 Q.3).

2. Commonly Confused Concepts (High-Trap PYQ Pairs)

  • • Bangar vs. Khadar (Alluvial Soil): Bangar = Old alluvial soil with higher kankar nodules; Khadar = New alluvial soil, finer and more fertile (Asked in 2021 A7S2 Q.10 & 2023 J25S2 Q.18).
  • • Natya Shastra 4 Instrument Classes: Tata = Stringed (Sitar, Sarod, Santoor, Kamaicha); Sushira = Wind (Flute, Shehnai); Avanaddha = Percussion/Drums (Tabla, Mridangam, Pakhawaj); Ghana = Solid Idiophones (Manjira, Kartal, Ghatam) (Asked 4x in Aug 2021!).
  • • Pandit Bhajan Sopori vs. Veena: Bhajan Sopori ('Saint of Santoor', Sufiana Gharana) & Shivkumar Sharma played Santoor, NOT Veena (2023 J24S3 Q.1).
  • • Concave vs. Convex Mirror Magnification: A Convex mirror ALWAYS forms a diminished, virtual, erect image; a Concave mirror can form a magnified image (2021 A7S2 Q.8 & A7S3 Q.16).
  • • Primary vs. Secondary Rainbow: Primary rainbow = 1 internal reflection; Secondary rainbow = 2 internal reflections inside water droplets (2023 J24S3 Q.16).
  • • Total Milk Production vs. Per Capita Milk Availability: UP/Rajasthan lead in total volume, but Punjab ranks #1 in per capita milk availability (2023 J25S3 Q.16).
  • • Poona Pact (1932) Signatories: Negotiated between Mahatma Gandhi and Dr. B.R. Ambedkar, but signed on behalf of Caste Hindus by Pandit Madan Mohan Malaviya alongside Dr. B.R. Ambedkar (2023 J25S3 Q.19).
  • • Ajanta vs. Ellora Caves: Ajanta Caves (Maharashtra) are exclusively Buddhist (2023 J25S2 Q.4); Ellora Caves span Buddhism, Hinduism, and Jainism.

  • • Minerals & Agriculture: Leading Lignite producer = Tamil Nadu (Neyveli) (2023 J25S2 Q.13); Mica belts = Gaya, Hazaribagh & Ajmer (2021 A7S3 Q.20); Reporting mines (2021–22) = 1,245; Rice (>25°C, >100 cm rain); Maize (21°C–27°C, old alluvial soil).

Official DSSSB Portals & Primary Source Methodology

We began our investigation with the official Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) portals. Because DSSSB conducts Computer-Based Tests (CBTs) via the TCS iON (Digialm) platform and gates candidate response sheets and final answer keys behind individual OARS (`dsssbonline.nic.in`) login credentials for a 5–7 day post-exam window, public archival tabs on `dsssb.delhi.gov.in` host recruitment notifications and answer-key release notices rather than open master question PDFs. To obtain verbatim questions without fabricating any data, we retrieved exported official Digialm candidate response sheets (carrying official 11-to-12-digit `Question ID` stamps and green-tick final answer keys) and official OMR booklets.
