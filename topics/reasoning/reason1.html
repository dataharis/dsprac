📄 DSSSB Reasoning Deep Research Report & Deployment Guide (2021–2026)

1. Official DSSSB Examination Scheme (PRT, TGT, PGT & TGT Computer Science)

• Section-A General Intelligence & Reasoning Ability: 20 Questions carrying 20 Marks out of the 100-mark General Paper (Total Paper = 200 Marks, 120 Minutes, Composite Time).

• Negative Marking: 0.25 marks deducted for each wrong MCQ response.

• Sectional Cutoff Rule: Minimum qualifying marks (40% UR, 35% OBC Delhi, 30% SC/ST) apply as per DSSSB notification norms; high scores in Reasoning (18–20/20 in ≤14 mins) are decisive for final merit.

2. Empirical Shift-Wise Topic Distribution (2021–2026 Official Papers)

  • Series (Number + Letter Cluster): 3 to 4 Questions per shift (Alternating differences like 88, 87, 89, 86, 90, ?; Second-tier difference; Progressive letter jumps).
  • Coding-Decoding & Analogy / Odd One Out: 4 to 5 Questions per shift (4-letter split progressive shift SQUIRREL→WXEVVYOY; Alphanumeric cluster PV7:SY14::GT9:JW18; Substitution coding 'BOTTLE is called ZERO'; Letter-pair gap CE-FH vs IK-LO).
  • Syllogism & Venn Diagrams: 3 Questions per shift (Disjoint negative statements 'No A is K, No K is W'; Complementary Either-Or; Region sum 'How many cars are tea? = 44+8=52').
  • Seating Arrangement & Logical Puzzles: 2 to 3 Questions per shift (6–7 person circular/linear rows; 7-Day Mon–Sun exam schedule; Conditional 7-of-10 team selection M1–M10).
  • Blood Relations & Direction-Distance: 2 to 3 Questions per shift (Coded expression P×Q−R+S; 6-member family tree; Sunrise shadow to Chanu's left; Multi-turn North/South cancellation).
  • Mathematical Operations & Data Sufficiency: 2 to 3 Questions per shift (Hidden operators 18 @ 27 # 2 = 72; Symbol replacement BODMAS; 2-statement Data Sufficiency on village distance or height comparison).
  • Non-Verbal Reasoning: 2 to 3 Questions per shift (Open dice folding opposite-face elimination; Embedded figures; Pattern completion; Triangle/Square counting).
