Easy-Scoring Speed Topics (Solve in < 25 Seconds Each)

  • 1. Alphabet & Alphanumeric Series (3–4 Qs/shift): Track 1st and 2nd letters via EJOTY positions (+3, +5, –7, –9 or –2 uniform shifts).
  • 2. Common-Word/Letter Elimination Coding (1–2 Qs/shift): Cancel shared digits between two 4-letter words (e.g., TYRE=5127, TREK=7253 ⇒ K=3) in 3 seconds.
  • 3. Operator Substitution & Number Interchange (2–3 Qs/shift): Use the Non-Integer Division Filter (`÷`) and Fraction Cancellation (`38/57 × 9 = 6`).
  • 4. Linear Ranking & Order (1–2 Qs/shift): Direct application of `L = T – R + 1` and Overlapping Mid `(L + R) – T – 2`.

Practice-Intensive Topics (Watch Out for DSSSB Traps)

  • 1. Multi-Variable Scheduling & Seating Puzzles (2–3 Qs/shift): E.g., 6 teachers Mon–Sat with married couples (1 Aug 2021) or 7/8 friends circular seating. Read the question stem first — often 2 local clues solve it immediately!
  • 2. Statement & Conclusion / Assumption (2–3 Qs/shift): High trap rate due to moral/real-world bias (e.g., 'Health is the best wealth' or celebrity news). Never assume anything outside the text.
  • 3. Number Classification with Shared Multipliers (1–2 Qs/shift): When all 4 pairs multiply by `×7` (25 June 2023 Q.1), check Prime vs. Composite on the first number!
  • 4. Conditional Number Series Counting (1 Q/shift): Convert 'Even followed by Odd, not preceded by Even' into `[ODD – EVEN – ODD]` before scanning.

3. Coding-Decoding & Blood Relation Shortcuts

  • Split-Half Reversal (8 letters): STOP | WARS → POTS | SRAW (TGT CS 07 Feb 2024 Q.5).
  • Pairwise Cross +1 Shift (6 letters): MA | TT | ER → (A+1,M+1) = BN | UU | SF (TGT CS 25 June 2023 Q.19).
  • Alphabet Sum ÷ Constant: LOMQG = (12+15+13+17+7)/4 = 64/4 = 16 (TGT CS 07 Feb 2024 Q.2).
  • Common-Letter Cancellation: TYRE=5127, TREK=7253 → Cancel {T,R,E}={5,2,7} ⇒ K=3 (TGT CS 12 Sept 2025 Q.11).
  • Coded Blood Relation Tree Symbols: Male = [+], Female = [–], Spouse = ⇔, Sibling = —. Always check the operator after the second person if their gender is needed (e.g., S + T means S is Mother [–]).

4. Direction, Morning Shadow & Ranking Formulas

Morning Sunrise Shadow Shortcut (TGT CS 2023 Q.17):
• Morning Sun = East ⇒ All Shadows fall West.
• If A's shadow falls in front of B ⇒ B is facing East.
• If A's shadow falls to the left of B ⇒ B is facing North (if to the right of B ⇒ facing South).
1. Basic Rank Conversion: Total (T) = Left (L) + Right (R) – 1
2. Non-Overlapping Mid (L + R < T): Mid = T – (L + R)
3. Overlapping Mid (L + R > T): Mid = (L + R) – T – 2
4. Position Interchange Total: T = (New Pos of A) + (Old Pos of B) – 1

6. Clocks, Calendars & Unfolded Cube/Dice Nets

  • Clock Angle Formula: θ = | 30H – (11/2)M |. Hands coincide every 65 5/11 mins (11 times in 12 hrs, 22 times in 24 hrs). Right angles (90°) occur 22 times in 12 hrs (44 times in 24 hrs).
  • Calendar Odd Days: Ordinary Year (365 days) = 1 odd day; Leap Year (366 days) = 2 odd days. Century odd days: 100 yrs = 5, 200 yrs = 3, 300 yrs = 1, 400 yrs = 0 odd days.
  • Unfolded Cube/Dice Net Rule: In any straight strip of 3 or 4 squares, alternate squares (skipping 1 square) are ALWAYS opposite faces. Two opposite faces can NEVER touch or appear together in a folded 3D view!
  • Triangle Counting Formula: Vertex lines dividing base into n segments with H horizontal cross-lines = [ n(n+1)/2 ] × H.
